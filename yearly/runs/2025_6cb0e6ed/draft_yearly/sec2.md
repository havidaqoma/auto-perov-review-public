Conflicting investigations across the year dispute how surface molecular passivation and low-dimensional heterojunctions behave at wide-bandgap (WBG) perovskite interfaces. One camp holds that they eliminate non-radiative recombination without transport penalties, while the other holds that they induce severe electrostatic crosstalk. Capping WBG absorbers with three-dimensional/quasi-two-dimensional perovskite heterojunctions suppresses halide segregation and passivates interface defects, which minimizes open-circuit voltage (Voc) deficits. That stack delivers an independently certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 28.81% in two-terminal (2T) tandems [@10.1002/adma.202504321], and a related device reached 28.52% certified in an unknown device class [@10.1126/sciadv.adv4501]. Modulating competitive adsorption of hybrid self-assembled monolayer (SAM) molecules at buried contacts aligns interfacial energy levels, achieving a certified 2T tandem PCE of 28.78% [@10.1038/s41467-025-58111-y]. Directional nucleation via ammonium additives yields 28.06% in 2T tandems [@10.1002/adma.202513281]. In sharp disagreement, competing reports demonstrate that asymmetric cation surface treatments generate electrostatic potential distortions and chemical crosstalk that impede carrier extraction across hole-selective contacts. Those reports conclude that only self-consistent cation-anion integration eliminates this crosstalk, reaching a certified 2T tandem PCE of 31.47% [@10.1002/anie.202509782]. Whether surface modifiers permanently eliminate deep traps or merely establish transient electrostatic screening remains open, and settling it requires operando, bias-dependent drive-level capacitance profiling (DLCP) paired with intensity-modulated photovoltage spectroscopy across functional junctions under operating electrical load [@10.1002/anie.202509782] [@10.1002/adma.202504321].

A parallel divergence divides investigations of narrow-bandgap mixed tin-lead (Sn-Pb) subcells. At issue is whether operational degradation is governed primarily by mobile halide defect migration or by mechanical microstrain. Chemical immobilization strategies treat vacancy-assisted ion drift as the primary failure mode, and on that view coordinating mobile halides and metal centers suppresses defect generation, achieving certified 2T tandem PCE values of 28.31% [@10.1038/s41467-025-58810-6] and 27.17% [@10.1021/acsenergylett.4c03370]. Competing studies demonstrate instead that chemical passivation alone cannot prevent degradation, because lattice mismatch and localized microstrain accelerate phase decomposition. Introducing lattice-stabilizing networks to homogenize internal strain and decouple photothermal-mechanical breakdown yields a certified PCE of 28.9% in 2T tandems [@10.1038/s41467-025-64274-5]. Related networks reach 28.11% in 2T tandems [@10.1038/s41467-025-56549-8] and 29.2% in an unknown device class [@10.1038/s41467-025-62661-6]. Differentiating chemical defect migration from lattice strain relaxation as the rate-limiting degradation pathway remains open, and doing so requires operando high-resolution synchrotron X-ray diffraction (XRD) topography correlated with depth-resolved time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectrometry (ToF-SIMS) under simultaneous electrical bias and thermal stress [@10.1038/s41467-025-62661-6] [@10.1038/s41467-025-64274-5] [@10.1038/s41467-025-58810-6].

Conformal growth on textured substrates reveals a third divergence between physical substrate modification and chemical crystallization control. One route abandons standard commercial textures: re-engineering industrially textured silicon into iceberg-like rounded pyramids enables uniform solution wetting and conformal absorber deposition, establishing the annual certified record of 33.15% in 2T tandems [@10.1038/s41467-025-62389-3]. Alternative approaches show that altering substrate topography is unnecessary if crystallization kinetics and nucleation dynamics are modulated chemically. Oriented grains on unflattened textures achieve certified 2T tandem efficiencies of 31.4% [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c01244], 30.26% [@10.1038/s41467-024-55377-6], and 27.92% [@10.1038/s41467-025-62391-9]. Monolithic integration with copper indium gallium selenide (CIGS) bottom subcells introduces inter-subcell mechanical coupling. Here substrate corrugation relieves tensile stress to achieve a certified 2T tandem PCE of 28% [@10.1021/jacs.5c13264]. Composite intermediate recombination layers achieve certified 2T tandem efficiencies of 30.1% [@10.1038/s41467-025-67350-y] and 25.45% [@10.1002/anie.202504237]. However, macroscale area expansion drops certified 2T tandem efficiency to 28.9% [@10.1038/s41467-025-63673-y]. Certified modules fall further, to 25.2% [@10.1002/adma.202505475] and 22.06% [@10.1002/adma.202419750]. That spread exposes the persistent gap between small-area laboratory cells and scalable tandems [@10.1038/s41467-025-62389-3] [@10.1038/s41467-025-63673-y].

Between the first month with data (30.26% in 2025-01 for 2T tandems) [@10.1038/s41467-024-55377-6] and the final month (30.1% in 2025-12 for 2T tandems) [@10.1038/s41467-025-67350-y], the net change in best certified efficiency was -0.16 percentage points. The monthly series displays neither steady progress nor monotonic stagnation, showing instead punctuated dispersion across 66 certified reports out of 450 closely read papers. Certified monthly maximums dropped to 27.17% in 2025-02 (4 certified papers, 2T tandem) [@10.1021/acsenergylett.4c03370], and they rebounded to 28.78% in 2025-03 (6 certified papers, 2T tandem) [@10.1038/s41467-025-58111-y] and 28.31% in 2025-04 (4 certified papers, 2T tandem) [@10.1038/s41467-025-58810-6]. Certified reporting then ceased in 2025-05 (0 certified papers), before reaching 28.81% in 2025-06 (3 certified papers, 2T tandem) [@10.1002/adma.202504321]. After dropping to 26.56% in 2025-07 (8 certified papers, inverted p-type-intrinsic-n-type (p-i-n) single-junction) [@10.1016/j.esci.2025.100451], certified performance peaked at 33.15% in 2025-08 (6 certified papers, 2T tandem) [@10.1038/s41467-025-62389-3]. It then retreated through 31.47% in 2025-09 (7 certified papers, 2T tandem) [@10.1002/anie.202509782], 28.9% in 2025-10 (7 certified papers, 2T tandem) [@10.1038/s41467-025-64274-5], and 26.96% in 2025-11 (7 certified papers, p-i-n single-junction) [@10.1038/s41566-025-01791-1], closing at 30.1% in 2025-12 (5 certified papers, 2T tandem) [@10.1038/s41467-025-67350-y]. Concurrently, certified single-junction p-i-n devices converged between 26.79% [@10.1002/adma.202505115], 26.74% [@10.1002/anie.202505876], and 26.65% [@10.1038/s41467-025-63389-z]. Uncertified self-reported claims outpaced certified metrics in eleven of twelve months, and they ran up to 33.2% in 2025-05 and 33.5% in 2025-12 (both in unclassified device architectures), coinciding with certified data only in 2025-08 at 33.15%. Crucially, multiple publications carry only the year 2025 without month designations. Those papers are excluded from the series, which therefore does not represent complete coverage of annual output.
