Wide-bandgap (WBG) perovskite top cells coupled to narrow-bandgap bottom absorbers suffer from photoinduced phase segregation. Studies across the year reach incompatible conclusions regarding the governing physical mechanism. One camp contends that crystallographic orientation dictates halide demixing by modifying ion-migration energy barriers. Trioctylphosphine oxide additives induce preferential (111) texturing, reported to elevate ion-migration activation energy and suppress phase separation relative to the (100) facet, yielding a certified two-terminal (2T) power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 30.26% (champion 30.78%) on textured silicon, with stability exceeding 1,000 hours [@10.1038/s41467-024-55377-6]. In contrast, bidentate anchoring with methylammonium chloride derivatives (MAMCl) directs preferential (100) nucleation, asserting instead that (100) orientation relieves lattice strain to suppress non-radiative defects in 1.77 eV absorbers. Its tandem achieved a certified 2T tandem PCE of 28.06% (champion 29.0%) with an open-circuit voltage (Voc) of 1.369 V and 20.4% single-junction efficiency [@10.1002/adma.202513281]. Rejecting crystallographic texture as secondary, competing reports argue that phase stability is governed by chemical alloying or ionic site competition. Melamine additives in rubidium-alloyed lattices suppress phase separation and enhance carrier mobility, yielding a 1.68 eV cell with 25% PCE, 1.31 V Voc, and 86.4% fill factor (FF). That cell enabled an uncertified champion 2T perovskite/silicon tandem PCE of 33.5% (stabilised at 33.5%) with an International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability (ISOS) ISOS-L-2 T80 lifetime of 3,200 hours [@10.1038/s41467-025-66480-7]. Concurrently, ionic site competition directs Cl- anions into lattice rather than interstitial sites, raising single-junction WBG efficiency to 23.88% and 2T tandem efficiency to 32.39% under continuous testing [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c02408]. Settling whether facet orientation, cation alloying, or interstitial occupancy governs phase segregation requires operando, depth-resolved grazing-incidence wide-angle X-ray scattering. That measurement must be paired with nanoscale secondary-ion mass spectrometry under 1-sun bias to decouple surface recrystallization from bulk halide redistribution.

Contradictory evidence also surrounds substrate morphology and residual mechanical stress in perovskite/copper indium gallium selenide (CIGS) tandems. Conventional wisdom assumed planarizing rough chalcopyrite was required to avoid shunts. However, polished CIGS substrates induce harmful tensile stress in perovskite films that weakens bonding and accelerates degradation [@10.1021/jacs.5c13264]. Retaining corrugated, rough CIGS topographies mechanically relaxes tensile stress, securing a certified 28% efficiency (stabilised at 28%) and an operational T80 of 1,123 hours [@10.1021/jacs.5c13264]. Conversely, flexible monolithic perovskite/CIGS tandems on polymer substrates achieve 22.8% PCE with Voc exceeding 1.8 V on smooth substrates. That result follows from modifying radio-frequency sputtered nickel oxide (NiOx) with [4-(3,6-dimethyl-9H-carbazol-9-yl)butyl]phosphonic acid derivatives (4PADCB) to form NiOx-P, which suppresses interface recombination without substrate corrugation [@10.1002/aenm.202403682]. In perovskite/silicon tandems, localized silicon oxide (SiOx) nanosphere contacts sit on industrial silicon with iceberg-like pyramids exceeding 2 μm, and those contacts permit complete solution coverage and mitigate interfacial recombination, delivering a certified 33.15% tandem PCE [@10.1038/s41467-025-62389-3]. Vapor-solution hybrid two-step crystallization modulation similarly enables conformal WBG growth on fully textured silicon for a certified 31.4% PCE [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c01244]. Meanwhile, 5C7A additives relieve internal stress and elevate the ion migration barrier, raising inverted single-junction PCE to 23.25% and textured silicon tandem PCE to 30.59% [@10.1002/solr.202500173].

Mechanistic divergence extends to hole-selective contacts based on self-assembled monolayers (SAMs), where one model asserts that maximizing permanent molecular dipoles drives rapid charge extraction through enhanced band bending. The Knoevenagel-condensed molecule BCPA possesses an electrostatic dipole moment of 18.16 Debye, far exceeding the 1.60 Debye of [4-(3,6-dimethyl-9H-carbazol-9-yl)butyl]phosphonic acid (Me-4PACz), and that molecule raised single-junction efficiency to 19.08% and perovskite/organic tandem efficiency to 26.10% [@10.1002/anie.202518169]. Asymmetric fluorinated SAMs with enlarged dipoles similarly boost perovskite/passivated emitter and rear cell (PERC) tandems beyond 30% efficiency with a 30.05% champion PCE [@10.1039/d5ee02650d]. Conversely, competing evidence indicates that anchoring uniformity outweighs dipole magnitude. Blending 4-aminobenzylphosphonic acid with Me-4PACz constructs a synergistic SAM that reinforces buried anchoring, yielding an inverted single-junction certified PCE of 25.45% (champion 25.75%) and a 2T tandem PCE of 31.56% on 1.07 cm² [@10.1002/anie.202504237]. Bifacial reinforcement via 4-PhCz improves SAM coverage on indium tin oxide (ITO), and the 1.67 eV single junction reached a Voc of 1.273 V with a voltage loss of 0.397 V (22.53% PCE), alongside a 31.26% 2T tandem [@10.1002/adma.202504520]. Super-hydrophilic chlorine-substituted molecules deliver 23.97% single-junction and 31.12% tandem efficiencies via enhanced wetting [@10.1002/aenm.202502222]. Micelle-assisted SAM deposition yields 20.95% single-junction and 29.8% tandem PCE on 1 cm², retaining 92% performance after 300 hours of maximum power point (MPP) tracking under unfiltered Xenon illumination [@10.1002/aenm.202405675]. Inorganic interlayers further modulate SAM interfaces: Ti3C2Tx MXene nanosheets form Ti-O-P bonds that raise the MeO-2PACz work function [@10.1021/acsanm.5c00290], and Nb-MXene paired with S-benzyl-L-cysteine reduces valence band offsets [@10.1002/smll.202411310]. TbCl3 doping at the Me-4PACz/CsPbI3 junction boosts all-inorganic single junctions to 18.68% and silicon-based tandems to 29.40% in four-terminal (4T) and 25.44% in 2T configurations [@10.1007/s40820-025-01763-8]. Multi-site agents passivating NiOx, the buried interface, and silicon edges simultaneously achieve a 31.02% 4T tandem [@10.1016/j.esci.2025.100471].

At the interconnecting layer (ICL) coupling subcells, optical parasitism conflicts with ohmic carrier recombination across every tandem chemistry surveyed. In perovskite/CIGS tandems, an Al:ZnO/Au/NiOx/4PADCB composite ICL uses an ultrathin Au layer to accelerate recombination and a hybrid NiOx/4PADCB hole transport layer (HTL) to align energy levels, attaining a certified 30.1% PCE (champion 30.71% on 0.15 cm²) [@10.1038/s41467-025-67350-y]. In contrast, BMIMTFSI ionic liquid integration into SAMs achieves 28.34% in 4T perovskite/CIGS tandems (22.53% WBG and 25.68% normal-bandgap cells) [@10.1002/adma.202412692]. In lead-free tandems combining tin perovskites with CIGS, Pd(dppf)Cl2 functionalization delivers an 11.52% single-junction and 14.51% tandem PCE [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c00163]. Dual-interface passivation with succinic acid at SnO2 and monoethyl succinate at copper indium disulfide (CIS) subcells instead reduces diode ideality factors from 2.25 to 1.26 to yield 18.76% PCE [@10.1021/acsaem.5c02901]. In perovskite/organic tandems, tailoring Au nanoparticle dimensions in n-i-p ICLs suppresses plasmonic parasitic absorption, providing a current gain exceeding 1.5 mA/cm² in the organic rear cell and a 25.34% tandem PCE [@10.1038/s41467-024-55376-7]. Alternatively, metal-free SAM/MoO3/SAM junctions convert the molybdenum oxide surface from n-type to p-type to yield 26.05% PCE [@10.1038/s41467-025-58047-3]. Similarly, 1,2,4-trichlorobenzene additives enforce face-on SAM packing for 26% tandems [@10.1039/d4ee05533k], while sputtered zinc tin oxide (ZTO) provides an indium-free recombination layer [@10.1002/smll.202511646]. Operational degradation in perovskite/organic devices arises from C60 electron transport layer (ETL) recrystallization, which opens grain boundaries to halide and silver interdiffusion. Organic barrier layers suppress this corrosion to sustain 25.5% efficiency [@10.34734/fzj-2025-05790]. Polymerizable additives in 1.86 eV WBG absorbers retain 84% of initial efficiency after 1,000 hours of maximum power point tracking (MPPT) in 25.06% tandems [@10.1002/adfm.202502422]. Dual passivation with MESK and octylammonium iodide yields 23.69% in 4T tandems [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.5c00756]. Interface modeling indicates that PbI-terminated MAPbI3/poly(3-hexylthiophene) (P3HT) heterojunctions provide stronger orbital hybridization and valence band alignment than MAI terminations [@10.48550/arxiv.2512.22321].

Top-interface passivation and module scaling introduce further mechanistic trade-offs, beginning with cation competition at the perovskite surface. Pairing 1,3-propanediamine with trifluoroacetate counterions in PDA(TFA)2 suppresses that competition, delivering 23.23% in 1.68 eV single-junction cells with 1.27 V Voc, and the same route gave a certified 31.47% tandem PCE (champion 32.33% on 0.945 cm²) [@10.1002/anie.202509782]. Bidirectional ion transport from piperazinium chloride passivates top and buried interfaces simultaneously, reaching 22.3% in single junctions with a Voc × FF product at 84.4% of the Shockley-Queisser limit. The same passivator gave 31.5% in 2T tandems on 1.04 cm² with a T80 of 755 hours [@10.1007/s40820-024-01631-x]. Cooperative dipole reconstruction with acetylcholine chloride and n-octylammonium iodide raises Voc to 1.29 V, capturing 92.8% of the Shockley-Queisser voltage limit in a 21.27% tandem [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c02738]. In 3D/quasi-2D Dion-Jacobson heterostructures, combined evaporation and solution deposition yields a certified 20.53% single-junction PCE (champion 20.71%) and a certified 28.81% tandem PCE (champion 28.99%) with 501 hours T80 [@10.1002/adma.202504321]. Across large areas, combining phosphonic acids with piperazinium chloride increases quasi-Fermi level splitting by approximately 100 mV, enabling certified 28.9% efficiency on 60 cm² [@10.1038/s41467-025-63673-y]. VBETS passivators instead deliver 30.98% tandems and 21.00% across 32.144 cm² modules [@10.1038/s41467-025-56068-6]. At silicon contacts, oblique-angle evaporation deposits conformal SiOx electron contacts without damaging underlying perovskite in tunnel oxide passivated contact (TOPCon) tandems [@10.1002/aenm.202570020]. Ultrathin poly(3,4-ethylenedioxythiophene) polystyrene sulfonate (PEDOT:PSS) sacrificial layers instead prevent resistive SiOx growth during annealing, maintaining a 0.813 FF in 21.95% tandems [@10.1186/s40580-025-00492-3]. Sulfonium DMPESI molecules form internal hydrophobic encapsulation layers that suppress degradation during air-annealing, and those devices retained 84% efficiency after nearly 1,800 hours of MPP tracking under ISOS-L-1 in 30.49% tandems on 1.21 cm² (T80 of 723 hours) [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c01010]. MXene grain boundary additives dissipate heat to yield 25.13% PCE with 500 hours T80 [@10.1007/s40820-025-01855-5]. Certified single-junction perovskite efficiency reached 27.3%, and hybrid perovskite/silicon tandems approach 35% in small-area cells [@10.1021/acs.accounts.5c00612]. These values remain substantially below the theoretical detailed-balance limit of 47.6% for two-junction tandems. Simulated annual energy yields scale identically with top-cell bandgap across geographies, indicating that a twofold relaxation in perovskite degradation rates is sufficient to match silicon levelized cost of electricity [@10.48550/arxiv.2502.05465].
