Automated regular-expression detection over abstracts identifies what authors state in title-level summaries rather than verifying experimental execution within full texts. Across 4730 abstracts, the detection rate for power conversion efficiency (PCE) was 51.6% pooled over summed denominators, remaining flat across the year with a narrow monthly range of 47.8% to 54.0%. This confirms that steady-state photovoltaic conversion serves as the standard self-reported metric. In contrast, the detection rate for independently certified efficiency was only 5.8% of 4730 abstracts, fluctuating between 3.4% and 7.6% across monthly cohorts. Such a restricted detection rate leaves champion claims unverified against potential spectral mismatch, stray-light artifacts, or calibration drift. Rigorous independent certification benchmarks high-performance devices, as demonstrated by certified values of 26.03% in single-junction cells [@10.1002/aenm.202502409], 25.18% on inverted contacts [@10.1038/s41467-025-55815-z], and 28.31% in monolithic tandems [@10.1038/s41467-025-58810-6], yet these verified results remain rare exceptions within the broader literature.

The gap between operational steady-state performance and dynamic current-voltage (J-V) sweeps is highlighted by the low detection rate for stabilised efficiency, which pooled at 8.8% of 4730 abstracts. Unlike the flat rate observed for raw efficiency, stabilised efficiency detection swung across the year from a minimum of 6.2% to a maximum of 12.0%, reflecting an accelerating but incomplete shift toward continuous maximum power point tracking (MPPT). Without stabilised power outputs, rapid voltage sweeps routinely record transient capacitive charge collection and mobile ion redistribution rather than electronic equilibrium. This diagnostic ambiguity is exacerbated by the near-total absence of hysteresis disclosures, detected in only 3.1% of 4730 abstracts with a flat monthly range of 1.6% to 4.3%. Because mobile halide vacancies accumulate at contact interfaces under external bias to create internal screening fields, unstated scan-rate-dependent hysteresis obscures carrier extraction kinetics. Where hysteresis is explicitly examined and resolved, it correlates directly with targeted defect elimination, such as dual-anchor interfacial passivation [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.5c02124], organic potassium salt chelation [@10.1002/smll.202511139], or B-site lattice doping that elevates migration barriers against mobile iodide drift [@10.1126/sciadv.ads7054] [@10.1038/s41467-025-58810-6].

Device active area was stated in only 7.3% of 4730 abstracts, remaining tightly bounded between 5.2% and 9.3% throughout the year. Omitting geometric dimensions across the overwhelming majority of abstracts conceals whether efficiency breakthroughs reflect scalable manufacturing or edge-enhanced collection on sub-0.1 cm2 apertures. High certified efficiencies frequently concentrate on small active areas of 0.09 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-025-55815-z] [@10.1038/s41467-025-64728-w]. When active area expands, non-uniform drying during meniscus coating, local shunt pinholes, and transparent conducting oxide sheet resistance induce severe geometric efficiency drops, decreasing from 26.40% on 0.09 cm2 to 23.08% on a 12.96 cm2 mini-module [@10.1038/s41467-025-64728-w]. Although bilateral interfacial bonding and rapid laser annealing achieve certified efficiencies of 26.65% across 764 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-025-63389-z] and 24.0% across 100 cm2 [@10.1126/science.adx9650], such large-area verifications remain exceptional amid widespread silence regarding cell geometry.

The thinnest reporting metric across the annual corpus was the International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability (ISOS) protocol label, detected in merely 1.1% of 4730 abstracts. Although this detection rate ranged monthly from 0.2% to 3.2%, the pooled rate of 1.1% demonstrates that standardized aging methodologies remain virtually absent from high-level dissemination. Unstandardized degradation tests fail to distinguish extrinsic encapsulation failure from intrinsic lattice decomposition. In contrast, groups adhering to standardized protocols systematically isolate degradation pathways, deploying ISOS-L-1 continuous illumination tracking [@10.1002/adma.202511062] [@10.1038/s41467-025-64550-4] or ISOS-L-2 thermal-light stress [@10.1038/s41467-025-55815-z] [@10.1038/s41467-025-66480-7] to evaluate self-assembled monolayer (SAM) contacts [@10.1021/acsnano.5c07208] [@10.1002/anie.202505876]. Adopting standardized ISOS protocols alongside mandatory disclosure of aperture areas, stabilised tracking, and certified calibration is necessary to convert self-reported claims into reproducible scientific evidence.
