Investigations across the past year reveal a fundamental mechanistic disagreement over the primary degradation vector in low-bandgap tin-lead (Sn-Pb) perovskites. At issue is whether oxidation of Sn(II) to Sn(IV) is initiated by autonomous chemical redox reactions, or whether it is secondary to photothermal-mechanical lattice strain that generates defect pathways. One school of thought treats Sn(II) oxidation as an intrinsic chemical defect process driven by volatile halogen species and residual impurities. That view requires active reducing agents or self-healing redox shuttles to restore metal states [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c01995] [@10.1038/s41467-025-58810-6]. Tris(2-carboxyethyl)phosphine (TCEP) exemplifies this approach, operating as an in situ redox shuttle that coordinates metal cations to enable self-healing. The shuttle allows single-junction Sn-Pb perovskite solar cells (PSCs) to reach a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 23.86% and retain 90% of initial performance after 563 h of maximum power point tracking (MPPT), translating into an uncertified two-terminal (2T) tandem PCE of 28.86% [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c01995]. Similarly, hydrazine monohydrochloride bulk passivation immobilizes ions via hydrazinium (N2H5+) hydrogen bonding to iodide, while chloride fills iodide vacancies. That route achieves a 23.21% low-bandgap single-junction PCE, a certified 2T tandem efficiency of 28.31%, and an uncertified champion of 28.55% [@10.1038/s41467-025-58810-6]. Conversely, competing investigations demonstrate that Sn(II) oxidation is predominantly downstream of photothermal-mechanical strain, where local lattice distortions generate the vacancies that subsequently facilitate oxidation [@10.1038/s41467-025-62661-6] [@10.1038/s41467-025-64274-5] [@10.1038/s41467-025-56549-8]. Within this mechanical framework, introducing sodium 1,3,6-naphthalenetrisulfonate (NTS) forms Sn-trisulfonate coordination that strengthens Sn-I bonds and homogenizes strain. Reducing light-induced lattice distortions yields a single-junction Sn-Pb PCE of 23.2%, a certified 2T tandem PCE of 29.2%, and an uncertified champion of 29.6% [@10.1038/s41467-025-62661-6]. The polyamine ligand tris(4-aminophenyl)amine (TPTA) establishes an I-Sn-N coordination framework that decouples photothermal-mechanical degradation from chemical breakdown. The resulting cell reaches a 23.4% single-junction Sn-Pb PCE that retains 94.9% of initial performance after 950 h of maximum power point (MPP) tracking, with a certified 2T tandem efficiency of 28.9% and a champion of 29.6% [@10.1038/s41467-025-64274-5]. In methylammonium (MA)-free absorbers, rubidium acetate suppresses Sn(II) oxidation by alleviating internal microstrain rather than relying on sacrificial redox chemistry. That approach delivers a single-junction Sn-Pb PCE of 23.02% with an open-circuit voltage (VOC) of 0.897 V and a fill factor (FF) of 80.64%, which yields a certified 2T tandem PCE of 28.11% (champion 29.33%) [@10.1038/s41467-025-56549-8]. Whether lattice distortion precedes vacancy generation or chemical oxidation initiates structural relaxation is still unsettled, and settling the question requires operando depth-resolved synchrotron X-ray diffraction combined with transient chemical spectroscopy under simultaneous thermal, electrical, and optical stress. This measurement would reveal whether strain relief halts Sn(IV) accumulation in the absence of exogenous reducing agents, or whether chemical oxidation proceeds unimpeded through unstrained crystalline domains.

Crystallization control and buried substrate interfaces similarly divide current processing approaches [@10.1002/anie.202518800] [@10.1021/acsenergylett.4c03370] [@10.1038/s41467-025-57195-w]. Regulating the buried interface and crystal growth with SATS produces a 23.85% single-junction Sn-Pb PCE and an uncertified 28.74% 2T tandem PCE [@10.1002/anie.202518800]. Substrate hydrophobicity imposes distinct constraints: on poly(triarylamine) (PTAA), non-wetting impedes uniform nucleation, and Lewis base additives such as 1-benzoyl-3-phenylurea (BPU) or its sulfonyl derivative (BPSU) assemble precursor clusters that accelerate nucleation, delay crystal growth, and passivate buried interface defects. These additives yield a single-junction Sn-Pb PCE of 23.87% on PTAA, a certified tandem PCE of 27.17%, and an uncertified champion of 23.87% [@10.1021/acsenergylett.4c03370]. Heteroepitaxial seeding presents an alternative growth route: in situ reaction of potassium stannate with lead iodide forms lead stannate (PbSnO3), an ABX3-structured oxide providing a 98% lattice match [@10.1038/s41467-025-57195-w]. However, while PbSnO3 templates achieve an uncertified champion 2T tandem PCE of 28.81%, the stabilized efficiency is only 23.12%. Epitaxial matching therefore does not overcome severe transient losses or interfacial charge accumulation [@10.1038/s41467-025-57195-w].

Subcell current matching requires pairing the Sn-Pb absorber with a wide-bandgap (WBG) top cell absorbing between 1.75 and 1.80 eV [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c03339] [@10.1016/j.decarb.2025.100098]. A major debate centers on whether severe WBG VOC deficits arise from bulk halide phase segregation under illumination or from defect-mediated losses at contacts [@10.1021/acsnano.5c03542] [@10.1039/d4ee02898h] [@10.1126/sciadv.adv4501] [@10.1038/s41467-025-62391-9]. Supporting bulk segregation mitigation, the organic dye D358 reduces I2 to I- and oxidizes metallic Pb0 to Pb2+. Suppressing phase segregation raises WBG PCE from 18.75% to 19.94% and delivers an uncertified 28.83% tandem under International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability light soaking (ISOS-L-1) [@10.1021/acsnano.5c03542]. Multifunctional cross-linkers also suppress phase segregation and nonradiative recombination, yielding a certified WBG VOC of 1.37 V and an uncertified 28.3% tandem PCE [@10.1039/d4ee02898h]. In MA-free compositions, 1,3,5-trifluoro-2,4,6-triiodobenzene (TFBZ) forms N-H...F hydrogen bonds with formamidinium iodide (FAI). Those bonds retard crystallization and passivate iodine vacancies and undercoordinated Pb2+ sites, achieving a certified tandem PCE of 28.52% (champion 29.01%) [@10.1126/sciadv.adv4501]. In parallel, an ether-ring supramolecule suppresses light-induced halide segregation, enabling 1.77 eV cells to reach 21.01% PCE, retain 95% of initial efficiency over 1000 h of MPPT, and reach a certified tandem PCE of 27.92% (champion 28.44%) [@10.1038/s41467-025-62391-9].

In contrast to bulk segregation theories, substantial evidence attributes WBG open-circuit voltage deficits to unpassivated defects and dipole pinning at the buried hole extraction interface [@10.1021/acsnano.5c04978] [@10.1039/d5ee02696b] [@10.1002/aenm.202501556] [@10.1002/adfm.202500168] [@10.1038/s41467-025-59515-6] [@10.1039/d5ee01369k]. Sulfonic molecular bridges using sulfopropyl methacrylate (SPM) generate vertically aligned dipoles that accelerate charge extraction. The bridged device achieves a 1.77 eV WBG PCE of 19.48% with a VOC of 1.350 V, a VOC deficit of 0.420 V, and an uncertified 28.9% tandem PCE [@10.1021/acsnano.5c04978]. POEAX parallel dipoles similarly adjust energy alignment, yielding a 19.54% WBG PCE and an uncertified 28.92% tandem PCE [@10.1039/d5ee02696b]. Molecular bridging with 4F-HBM at the buried interface yields a 1.77 eV WBG device with 20.09% PCE and an 84.71% FF, exceeding control values of 18.47% and 82.53%. That device retains 85% of initial efficiency after 821 h of MPPT and supports a four-terminal (4T) tandem PCE of 28.71% [@10.1002/aenm.202501556]. Contact potential homogenization uses 3-aminopropanoic acid (3-APA) blended with [2-(3,6-dimethoxy-9H-carbazol-9-yl)ethyl]phosphonic acid (Me-4PACz) in a self-assembled monolayer (SAM). The blend delivers a VOC x FF product reaching 84.5% of the Shockley-Queisser (SQ) limit, achieving a champion p-i-n PCE of 22.4% [@10.1002/adfm.202500168]. In situ 5-ammonium valeric acid iodide (5-AVAI) molecular compensation repairs SAM-deficient interfaces and grain-boundary defects [@10.1039/d5ee01369k]. Interfacial chemistry also dictates chemical stability. Strongly acidic phosphonic acid SAMs corrode underlying nickel oxide (NiOx), whereas less-acidic boric acid anchoring eliminates NiOx degradation to form aggregation-free monolayers, enabling an uncertified 28.5% tandem PCE [@10.1038/s41467-025-59515-6]. Transitioning to scalable deposition highlights phase management. Blade-coating 1.77 eV absorbers induces a parasitic 2H hexagonal phase, but adding 4-aminobenzylphosphonic acid suppresses the 2H phase to promote direct 3C cubic crystallization. That coating achieves a certified WBG PCE of 19.72% (champion 20.35%) and a 4T tandem PCE of 28.46%, though on a 0.07 cm2 aperture [@10.1126/sciadv.ady3621].

Monolithic 2T integration depends on an interconnecting layer (ICL) that pairs current matching with negligible optical parasitism and low resistance [@10.1016/j.pmatsci.2025.101560] [@10.1039/d4ee06027j] [@10.1021/acsenergylett.4c03065] [@10.1039/d5ta04259c] [@10.1039/d5ee02462e]. One route replaces conventional Au/poly(3,4-ethylenedioxythiophene) polystyrene sulfonate (PEDOT:PSS) stacks with a graphene oxide layer supporting [2-(9H-carbazol-9-yl)ethyl]phosphonic acid (2PACz). The swap eliminates parasitic absorption and raises champion tandem PCE from 19.7% to 23.4% [@10.1021/acsenergylett.4c03065]. Omitting atomic layer deposition (ALD) and noble metals with a simplified recombination layer produces an uncertified 26.56% 2T tandem [@10.1039/d5ta04259c]. Controlling growth to expose binding-favourable facets suppresses interfacial recombination to enhance voltage [@10.1039/d5ee02462e]. Although monolithic all-perovskite tandems operate toward the two-junction detailed-balance limit of 47.6% [@10.1016/j.pmatsci.2025.101560], experimental certified efficiencies remain between 27.17% and 29.2% on laboratory-scale cells [@10.1021/acsenergylett.4c03370] [@10.1038/s41467-025-56549-8] [@10.1038/s41467-025-58810-6] [@10.1038/s41467-025-62391-9] [@10.1126/sciadv.adv4501] [@10.1038/s41467-025-64274-5] [@10.1038/s41467-025-62661-6]. Crucially, scaling to practical dimensions incurs severe penalties. Surface treatment with propane-1,3-diammonium iodide yields a 1.77 eV subcell VOC of 1.35 V and a PCE of 19.9%, but scaling the tandem to 1 cm2 reduces the champion PCE to 26.0% (at a 17.8% solar-to-hydrogen efficiency) [@10.1038/s41467-024-55654-4]. Theoretical projections reach 27.3% for dual-junction KSnI3/FASnI3, and 30.69% PCE with a VOC of 2.766 V and an FF of 84.18% in triple-junction KSnI3/FASnI3/ACZTSe tandems [@10.48550/arxiv.2511.06059], but these projections rely on idealized transport models that neglect the chemical oxidation, phase segregation, and sheet resistance observed in practical devices.
