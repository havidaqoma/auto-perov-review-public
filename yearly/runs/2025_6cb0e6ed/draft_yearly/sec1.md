Perovskite photovoltaic research in 2025 exposed stark disagreements where independent investigations reached incompatible conclusions regarding identical physical degradation pathways. While several studies identified mobile iodide accumulation at electrode interfaces as the primary driver of rapid delamination, others attributed initial performance losses to irreversible redox reactions with adjacent charge-transport layers that proceed independently of halide migration. These competing models remain unresolved because conventional ex situ characterization decouples applied electrical bias from local chemical transformation. Settling this conflict requires operando, depth-resolved chemical spectroscopy synchronized with local potential and impedance mapping under simultaneous operational bias and thermal stress. This divergence illustrates how the field's central challenge has shifted: progress is no longer defined by incremental gains in power conversion efficiency (PCE), but by the interdependent demands of verification, scalable area, and operating lifetime.

Across the published literature, 7931 indexed works met the scope gate and 450 were read closely. This review was compiled retrospectively from the complete 2025 publication record, not by monitoring the literature month by month during 2025. Within this intake, 12 months carry date-resolved works and 1089 works are dated only to the year, so they contribute to the corpus but not to any month-by-month series. Because this collection forms an initial edition without a prior yearly issue, no year-over-year comparisons are made.

The mechanistic evaluation follows four primary threads. The first addresses single-junction devices to identify where the remaining losses sit, establishing whether non-radiative recombination concentrates at buried substrate contacts, bulk defect states, or top extraction interfaces. The second examines all-perovskite tandems, evaluating tin-lead (Sn-Pb) stability and subcell matching to resolve how divalent tin oxidation competes with phase segregation under current-matched illumination. The third considers hybrid tandems by assessing the interfacial coupling of perovskite absorbers to silicon, copper indium gallium selenide (CIGS), and organic subcells, balancing optical management against processing damage and mechanical strain. The fourth examines modules and large-area devices through the fluid dynamics and crystallization kinetics of scaling the deposited film, where drying rates and solvent extraction govern defect distributions across large substrates.

Throughout each thread, this review distinguishes what the year's literature verified from what it asserted. Reported PCE values measured on sub-0.1 cm2 apertures without independent verification, lifetime projections derived without continuous maximum power point tracking under load, and passivating mechanisms inferred without operando confirmation are separated from claims supported by reproducible physical measurements.
