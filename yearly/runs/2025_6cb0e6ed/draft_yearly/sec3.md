Single-junction perovskite solar cells (PSCs) with a 1.55 electron-volt (eV) absorber bandgap face a theoretical Shockley-Queisser (SQ) limit of 29.4%, yet certified records stand at 26.96% power conversion efficiency (PCE) with an uncertified champion of 27.02% and stabilized output of 26.61% on a 1 cm2 aperture under continuous 1-sun maximum power point (MPP) tracking [@10.1038/s41566-025-01791-1]. Across 332 investigations evaluated this year in this device class, 278 reporting measured metrics, the field divides sharply over where the remaining ~2.4% absolute efficiency deficit resides. One body of evidence attributes open-circuit voltage (VOC) and fill factor (FF) deficits to the buried hole-selective contact, citing energetic misalignment, pinholes, and chemical degradation of carbazole-based self-assembled monolayers (SAMs). An opposing body contends contact extraction is optimized and that losses reside in the absorber bulk and at grain boundaries through point defects, uncoordinated lead ions (Pb2+), and residual strain. This divergence drives conflicting engineering: one camp modifies substrates while another leaves contacts untouched, achieving identical gains via bulk crystallization. Settling whether primary losses sit at the contact or in the bulk requires operando absolute-intensity photoluminescence (PL) spectroscopy and cross-sectional quasi-Fermi level splitting (QFLS) mapping under 1-sun operational bias. Correlating internal QFLS with external terminal voltage across operating cells, rather than uncontacted precursor films, is the only measurement that isolates interfacial recombination velocities from bulk non-radiative decay.

The dispute over the buried hole transport layer (HTL) contact exposes structural contradictions in molecular design. In inverted (p-i-n) architectures with SAMs on indium tin oxide (ITO) or nickel oxide (NiOx), one group asserts molecular rigidity is mandatory to enforce vertical alignment, packing density, and order. The rigid pyrene SAM PhPAPy adopts vertical orientation on ITO, yielding a certified reverse-scanning PCE of 26.74% and certified stabilized output of 26.12% under International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability (ISOS)-L-2 protocol [@10.1002/anie.202505876]. Rigid conjugated bisphosphonic acids such as TPA2P exhibit a deep highest occupied molecular orbital (HOMO) of -5.47 eV, strengthening ITO binding and suppressing pinholes to yield an uncertified 26.11% PCE [@10.1021/jacs.5c05801], while all-conjugated block copolymers providing rigid transport channels reach an uncertified 26.11% PCE [@10.1002/anie.202509065]. Conversely, another set of investigations concludes rigid backbones induce steric embrittlement and pinhole dewetting, arguing flexible spacers are needed to relieve interfacial stress. Combining flexible aliphatic heads with rigid aromatic linkers in (4-(diphenylamino)phenyl)phosphonic acid yielded an uncertified champion PCE of 26.21% on 0.0715 cm2, but collapsed to 24.49% on 1 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-025-62388-4]. Likewise, a flexible central substituent in pyridine derivative 2PYET achieved an uncertified 26.03% PCE on 0.09 cm2, but dropped to 24.45% on 1.0 cm2 [@10.1002/anie.202524079]. These severe efficiency drops upon scaling from sub-0.1 cm2 to 1 cm2 show that micro-cell records often mask spatial dewetting that rigid versus flexible designs fail to reconcile.

The chemical stability of the SAM-substrate junction presents a parallel loss pathway. On NiOx, redox reactions between electrophilic Ni3+ sites and phosphonic acids trigger de-anchoring and interfacial traps. Countering this by blending MeOF-4SHCz with MeOF-4PACz (4:1 weight ratio) forms sulfur-oxygen-nickel (S-O-Ni) bonds at Ni3+-rich regions, achieving a certified PCE of 26.28% (uncertified champion 26.5%) with T90 > 1000 hours [@10.1038/s41467-025-66421-4]. Covalent tridentate anchoring via (3,3,3-trifluoropropyl)triethoxysilane (3F-PTES) on NiOx eliminates hydroxyl defects and produces an energetic offset of 0.01 eV with the perovskite, achieving an uncertified 26.47% PCE [@10.1002/adma.202507730]. Pre-anchoring arginine on NiOx weakens SAM amphiphilicity, suppressing aggregation to yield an uncertified 26.67% PCE on a 0.049 cm2 aperture [@10.1002/adma.202513600]. Multi-component assembly targets aggregation similarly: co-assembling benzylphosphonic acid (BPPA) with Me-4PACz coordinates NiOx defects and chelates interfacial Pb2+ (uncertified champion 26.35%) [@10.1002/advs.202512117], while embedding covalent organic frameworks (COFs) PT-COF and Zn-PT-COF into MeO-4PACz disrupts aggregation via pi-pi and hydrogen bonding, achieving a certified 26.12% PCE (uncertified champion 26.39%) [@10.1002/anie.202519875]. Aggregation is also suppressed by molecular doping with MCC, delivering a certified 26.65% PCE (champion 26.78%) with stability exceeding 2000 h and bifacial module efficiencies of 15.51% and 14.64% [@10.1002/aenm.202503252], functionalized ITO nanoparticles with -OH groups resisting solvent rinsing under ISOS-L-2 testing (uncertified champion 26.44%) [@10.1002/adma.202517573], and co-assembling acetyl dl-carnitine chloride with Me-4PACz (uncertified champion 25.78%) [@10.1002/smll.202504930]. Photochemical degradation of phosphonic SAMs under ultraviolet light prompted polymeric networks such as Poly-2PACz, yielding an uncertified champion PCE of 26.0% with a T80 of 500 h under ultraviolet illumination [@10.1126/sciadv.adu3493]. Interfacial dipole engineering with 2DPAA enlarges the buried dipole moment to 5.10 D, securing a certified 26.02% PCE (uncertified champion 26.53%) [@10.1038/s41467-024-55653-5], while electrostatically enhanced SAM anchoring achieves a certified 26.23% (steady-state 25.42%) on 1 cm2 and 24.72% on a 20.7 cm2 module [@10.1002/adma.202514735].

Molecular interlayers and chelating bridges inserted between the SAM and perovskite absorber attempt to bridge interfacial mismatch. An aluminum glycinate interlayer coordinates unanchored P-OH and NiO-OH while chelating Pb2+, securing a certified 26.21% PCE (uncertified champion 26.74%) in 1.55 eV cells and 20.71% in 1.785 eV wide-bandgap cells [@10.1002/adma.202419413]. Organic bridge BFS aligns energy levels and chelates Pb2+, raising uncertified efficiency to 26.16% from a 24.49% control [@10.1002/adfm.202520372], while 1,3,5-triazine-2,4,6-tricarboxylic acid derivative (TDCA) bridges achieve an uncertified 26.15% PCE (26.20% for 1.55 eV and 21.65% for 1.77 eV absorbers) [@10.1002/adma.202518406]. Incorporating 1-benzyl-3-methylimidazolium tetrafluoroborate (BzMIMBF4) establishes pi-conjugated heterojunctions passivating interface traps (uncertified champion 26.45%) [@10.1039/d5ee06342f], while 2,3,5,6-tetrafluoro-7,7,8,8-tetracyanoquinodimethane (F4TCNQ) forms charge-transfer complexes that raise VOC to 1.202 V with an uncertified 25.91% PCE and 91% retention after 1000 h maximum power point tracking (MPPT) [@10.1002/anie.202514365]. Inserting 1,1-bis(4-di-p-tolylaminophenyl)cyclohexane (TAPC) increases FF from 83.2% to 85.2%, reaching an uncertified 26.05% PCE on rigid substrates and 24.39% on flexible substrates [@10.1002/anie.202516012], while phenylhydrazinium chloride passivates defects and retards crystallization to reach an uncertified 25.80% PCE from a 24.67% control [@10.1039/d4ee05466k]. In regular (n-i-p) devices, buried interface losses at tin oxide (SnO2) electron transport layer (ETL) substrates involve oxygen vacancies and poor wetting. L-citrulline molecular bridges achieve an uncertified 25.95% PCE on a 0.07065 cm2 aperture [@10.1002/anie.202508169]. Potassium trifluoro(sulfonatomethyl)borate (TFSK) passivates SnO2 oxygen vacancies and hydroxyls, reaching an uncertified 25.82% PCE [@10.1002/cnl2.70042], while dipole-functionalized molecules targeting dual-site buried defects yield an uncertified 25.85% PCE (24.79% on 1 cm2) [@10.1002/anie.202509529]. Whether these interlayers primarily suppress interface trap recombination or template superior crystal growth remains unresolved due to absent depth-resolved recombination lifetimes.

This uncertainty connects directly to absorber bulk losses and the contentious role of two-dimensional (2D) perovskite phases at grain boundaries. Two conflicting schools debate whether converting residual lead iodide (PbI2) into 2D phases benefits performance. The prevailing view treats PbI2 as a defect source requiring conversion into 2D phases to suppress non-radiative decay and block mobile ions. Guanfacine hydrochloride conversion of boundary PbI2 into 2D perovskite achieves an uncertified 26.19% PCE [@10.1002/anie.202516433]. 1-(pyren-1-yl)methanamine hydroiodide (PyPAI) semiconductor spacers induce columnar 2D stacks directing vertical crystallization, reaching an uncertified 26.41% PCE [@10.1002/adma.202511235]. Tilted-oriented 2D perovskites enhance conductivity and uniformity, securing a certified 26.3% PCE (uncertified champion 26.6%) with 90% retention after 1100 h MPPT [@10.1002/aenm.202503780], while sequential ligand treatment using 2,6-difluorobenzylammonium bromide (DFABr) at boundaries and 4-(aminomethyl)piperidinium bromide (4APPBr2) on surfaces yields an uncertified 26.06% PCE [@10.1038/s41467-025-66478-1]. For indoor photovoltaics, Dion-Jacobson/Ruddlesden-Popper (DJ/RP) dual 2D heterostructures using butylammonium iodide (BAI) achieve an uncertified 30.3% PCE with a twentyfold T90 lifetime gain [@10.1002/adma.202419573]. In direct conflict, an opposing study proves 2D perovskite phases introduce insulating out-of-plane transport barriers that impede extraction and promote charge accumulation. Introducing carboxymethyl-beta-cyclodextrin (CM-beta-CD) to suppress phenylethylammonium iodide (PEAI) deprotonation deliberately inhibited 2D phase formation, raising PCE to an uncertified 26.06% from 23.75% for control devices, with 91.2% retention after 1050 h at 25 °C and 25-35% relative humidity (RH) [@10.1021/acsaem.5c02987]. This contradiction shows that 2D phase engineering often trades carrier transport for chemical encapsulation. Settling this trade-off requires temperature-dependent transient photovoltage and drive-level capacitance measurements to quantify extraction barriers across boundaries.

Where 2D phase conversion is avoided, eliminating bulk non-radiative decay relies on additives that regulate crystallization, chelate defects, and relax strain. Dimethyl sulfide soft Lewis bases guide heterojunction formation via soft-soft interactions, securing a certified 26.48% PCE (uncertified champion 26.7%) and over 94% retention after 2000 h MPP tracking under ISOS-L-1 [@10.1038/s41467-025-64550-4]. Coordinating Pb2+ and hydrogen bonding with iodide (I-) and formamidinium (FA+) using sodium 2-amino-4-(methylsulfonyl)butanoate (NHMS) achieves a certified 26.35% PCE (uncertified champion 26.51%) [@10.1002/adfm.202510458], while dual-ligand regulation with PCH and PBD enforces homogeneous ion distributions to deliver a certified 26.33% PCE (uncertified champion 26.66%) [@10.1002/anie.202518592]. Inhibiting light-induced decomposition with a hindered amine light stabilizer (HASS) yields a certified 26.56% PCE (uncertified champion 26.74%) [@10.1016/j.esci.2025.100451]. Dual-site passivation with AGAD and AESM via vacuum flash evaporation eliminates boundary defects, reaching an uncertified 26.14% PCE with 90% retention after 1200 h MPPT [@10.1002/adfm.202504424]. Suppressing the non-perovskite 6H hexagonal intermediate with butylamine acetate (BAAc) promotes alpha-phase FAPbI3 crystallization, reaching an uncertified 26.12% PCE [@10.1002/adfm.202507934]. Introducing phthalocyanine derivative BE-ZnPc coordinates Pb2+, yielding a certified 26.05% PCE (uncertified champion 26%) with 92% retention after 550 h at MPP [@10.1002/adfm.202425443], while diammonium ligands CyDMADI and PhDMADI exploit electron-withdrawing cores to passivate bulk traps, reaching an uncertified 26.04% PCE [@10.1002/adfm.202424259]. Relieving lattice strain with 3-methylthio-1,2,4-triazine yields an uncertified 26.04% PCE (24% on flexible substrates) [@10.1021/acsnano.5c09464], whereas 4,4'-oxybis(benzenesulfonyl chloride) (OBSC) binds Pb2+ through Pb-O interactions to yield a certified 26.03% PCE (uncertified champion 26.39%) [@10.1002/aenm.202502409]. Modulating FA+ and methylammonium (MA+) hydrogen bonding with multisite amide derivative NUNB achieves a certified 26.03% PCE (uncertified champion 26.34%, 21.92% on rigid modules) [@10.1002/aenm.202503781]. Dual organic potassium salts omnidirectionally passivate defects, retaining 88.7% PCE after 1100 h at 85 °C with an uncertified 25.78% PCE [@10.1002/smll.202511139]. Decoupling lattice strain via in situ polymerization of poly(2-acrylamido-2-methylpropane sulfonic acid) (P-AMPS) provides grain spatial isolation, delivering an uncertified 25.78% PCE under ISOS-L-1 [@10.1002/adma.202511062]. Single-crystal PSCs produced via nucleation engineering achieve an uncertified benchmark PCE of 25.8% [@10.1002/anie.202500947]. The fact that single crystals lacking grain boundaries do not exceed polycrystalline thin-film records demonstrates that grain boundary area alone does not limit performance; point defects and contact extraction dominate.

The third critical loss zone sits at the top electron-selective contact in inverted devices, where fullerenes (buckminsterfullerene (C60) and phenyl-C61-butyric acid methyl ester (PCBM)) interface with perovskite and metal cathode layers. Under operational stress, C60 films suffer dimerization, pinholes, and low fracture toughness that permits halide ions to corrode the metal contact. Interfacial sodium-heptafluorobutyrate functionalization promotes a compact C60 layer blocking ion diffusion, delivering the certified record PCE of 26.96% (uncertified champion 27.02%, stabilized 26.61% on 1 cm2) [@10.1038/s41566-025-01791-1]. Supramolecular host-guest encapsulation with C8A suppresses ion migration to reach an uncertified champion PCE of 27.18% (certified 26.79%) in inverted cells, unsealed devices retaining 95% initial PCE after 1015 h at MPP, alongside 26.01% (certified 25.68%) for TiO2-based regular n-i-p devices [@10.1002/adma.202505115]. To overcome C60 mechanical fragility, ionic salt CPMAC increases interfacial toughness threefold, enabling an uncertified 26% PCE on 6 cm2 cells with ~2% degradation after 2100 h of 1-sun operation at 65 °C [@10.1126/science.adv4701]. In PCBM contacts, phosphate-substituted fullerene FuPE compacts stacking and boosts electron mobility to 0.183 cm2 V-1 s-1, securing an uncertified 26% PCE [@10.1002/anie.202502950]. At the perovskite/ETL junction, triphenylphosphine oxide (TPPO) coordinates Pb2+, yielding an uncertified 26.01% PCE with a VOC of 1.23 V and a minimal voltage deficit of 0.32 V for MA-free PSCs under continuous MPPT [@10.34734/fzj-2026-01040]. Cathode modification with dipolar Rh-Py establishes ohmic contact and coordinates Pb2+ to yield an uncertified 25.8% PCE [@10.1021/jacs.5c13145].

In regular n-i-p cells, where 2,2',7,7'-tetrakis(N,N-di-p-methoxyphenylamino)-9,9'-spirobifluorene (Spiro-OMeTAD) serves as the top HTL, losses stem from dopant migration and interface traps. Planar bipyridine-thiosumanene isomer 5BT exposes Lewis base sites accelerating hole extraction, securing a certified 26.12% PCE (uncertified champion 26.15%) with 94.0% retention under ISOS-L-2 [@10.1002/anie.202510255]. Electron-withdrawing pyridinecarboxaldehyde oxime (PO) ligands increase perovskite ionization potential to inhibit ion generation, reaching an uncertified 26.13% PCE [@10.1002/anie.202502117], while hole interface molecule MeS-TPA-Cbz-HAI coordinates interfacial defects with Spiro-OMeTAD to achieve an uncertified 25.83% PCE [@10.1002/anie.202513869]. HTL-free carbon-based perovskite solar cells (HTL-free C-PSCs) using lithium carbonate (Li2CO3) dual passivation achieve a VOC of 1.142 V and an uncertified champion efficiency of 33.2% [@10.1007/s40820-025-01775-4], although time-resolved confocal PL microscopy confirms unpassivated boundary traps at the perovskite/carbon interface cause severe non-radiative recombination, and this 33.2% efficiency reflects non-standard indoor or concentrated conditions. Numerical modeling of quasi-interdigitated back-contact (QIBC) PSCs predicts 26.67% PCE across 30 contact pairs [@10.1016/j.cej.2025.171437], SCAPS-1D simulations of CsPbI2Br with CuI predict 23.66% PCE (champion 26.13% simulated) [@10.20944/preprints202505.0921.v1], and simulations of oxide double-perovskite Ba2AsNbO6 predict 29.11% PCE [@10.1364/opticaopen.30738665], illustrating that idealized contacts can approach the SQ limit. However, physical devices remain throttled by the coupled interplay of 2D transport barriers, SAM de-anchoring, and fullerene mechanical degradation under operating stress.
