Tightening. Composition was addressed in 271 of 648 works (41.8%), 38 read closely; defects in 101 of 648 works (15.6%), 45 read closely. Because this is the first month in the series, no month-over-month comparison is possible. On the composition axis, the dominant theme is cation and halide control of phase stability in wide-bandgap mixed-halide absorbers. A review frames photo-induced phase segregation as the root cause of bandgap inhomogeneity, listing composition design, crystallization control, and strain modulation as countermeasures [@10.26599/nre.2026.9120259]. Experimental work converges on local halide anchoring: rubidium at the A-site induces lattice distortion that suppresses halide segregation and reduces mobile-ion concentration, giving a wide-bandgap sub-cell an open-circuit voltage of 1.46 V [@10.1002/adfm.76715], while Sr2+ localizes halide anions, prevents I2Br- triple halide formation, and yields 20.18% at 1.77 eV and 19.02% at 1.84 eV [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01113]. Additives act similarly: TPPA manages residual PbI2 and retains 90% of initial PCE after 750 h of MPPT versus 270 h for controls [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01370], and HPA builds spacer cations in situ through hydrogen bonds with FA+ to form a 2D layer, with retention of 95.5% and 90% after 3000 h at 30C in N2 and 800 h at 85C in N2 [@10.1002/advs.76508]. Concurrent 4D-STEM and EDX mapping shows that inactive hexagonal polytypes are predominantly driven by local cesium deficiencies [@10.1002/adma.74382]. Design guidance comes from first-principles ensembles predicting about 29% peak efficiency for CsPb1-xSnxBr3 at x > 0.70 [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01421] and an epsilon-field screen of 5043 compositions in 0.02 seconds [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15005541/v1]. Tin composition work advances on several fronts. A high-n (n = 10) quasi-2D ACI platform modified with methylammonium bromide produces a nearly two-order-of-magnitude increase in ambipolar carrier mobility [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01671], and 3-furoic acid decouples nucleation from growth in tin perovskites through coordination with SnI2 and hydrogen bonding with FAI [@10.1002/adfm.76731]. Flexible tin cells reach 15.56% PCE (14.67% certified) on 0.049 cm2 and 22.21% under 1000 lux [@10.1002/anie.7827890]. Lead-free studies include Bi substitution in CsPbI3 and CsPbBr3, which modulates bandgaps but evolves toward phase segregation because trivalent cation incorporation is thermodynamically unstable [@10.1016/j.solmat.2026.114597], hydrostatic strain tuning of CsGeF3 and CsSnF3 to 1.9 eV and 1.32 eV at 4% compression [@10.1002/ente.70574], and epitaxial CsSnI3 on Ag(100), where an interfacial SnIx layer templates terrace growth [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01749]. The defects literature is dominated by molecular passivation of undercoordinated Pb2+ and halide vacancies, with agreement that buried interfaces matter as much as top surfaces. An ultrathin SCP layer at the perovskite electron-contact interface delivers a certified 26.84% PCE and 90% retention after 1485 h of MPPT [@10.1002/aenm.71305]; the carbazole SAM BTP passivates undercoordinated Pb2+ via Lewis acid-base interactions and reaches 26.85% [@10.1002/anie.1864800]; dithiopyr bridges adjacent Pb2+ sites, relieves lattice strain, and supports 26.9% champion and 21.28% on 30 by 30 cm2 modules [@10.1002/adma.74033]; and AHBA regulates the SnO2 buried interface through a cross-linked network with 3.5 ps interfacial extraction [@10.1002/adfm.77373]. SHAP analysis of a curated SAM dataset identifies the head group as the dominant efficiency driver [@10.1002/adma.74060]. These studies disagree on the SAM tail: longer alkyl chains strengthen the interfacial dipole and raise open-circuit voltage, yet the same chains act as tunneling barriers that impede hole extraction [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01335]. Grain-boundary and additive passivation forms a second, consistent cluster. Additives incorporated during crystallization accumulate precisely at boundaries and reach 26.32% champion PCE [@10.1002/smll.74811]; BFDS and DMI polymerize in situ into dual-dynamic covalent networks, giving 22.00% in HTL-free carbon devices [@10.1002/smll.74903]; and amphoteric ionic liquids passivate both Lewis acidic and basic defects, with GUDHP on 1.84 eV material reaching 20.06% at 85.74% fill factor [@10.1002/adfm.77378]. mTFMA treatment raises average carrier lifetime from 529.7 to 2479.4 ns [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01560]; FTAC reconstructs the surface via Pb-N coordination and iodine-vacancy compensation with 91.16% retention after 1,000 h at 85C [@10.20517/energymater.2026.102]; TTA coordinates dangling bonds through its carboxyl group while sulfur binds free PbI2 [@10.1002/adma.73950]; and the 3D amino distribution of 4APM gives the strongest interaction with PbI2 and FAI among three candidates, with over 95% retention after 1000 h of MPPT at 65C [@10.1038/s41467-026-75990-x]. Choline chemistry appears twice: a choline-derivative anchoring ligand enables air-processed passivation with over 90% retention after 2000 h at 65C and 90% RH [@10.1002/adma.74243], and acetylthiocholine chloride fills vacancies and suppresses halide segregation in modules [@10.1002/ece2.70102]. Vacancy-specific and non-molecular results sharpen the mechanism. Pb-I diatomic and iodine single-atomic vacancies on Cs-rich, Pb-poor evaporated surfaces are the primary origin of Voc loss, and BaI2 vapor passivates them without phase-transforming lattice changes [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01161]. Nonadiabatic simulations show oxygen vacancies at the MAPbI3/NiO interface accelerate hole transfer while promoting nonradiative recombination [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c01907]. Plasma-based passivation forms conformal heterostructures on large-area films, with small-area devices retaining 98.1% of initial PCE after 2000 h of MPPT at 85C and a certified 23.5% on 65.05 cm2 modules [@10.1126/science.aeg1730]. A third theme links defects to strain: dithiopyr relieves lattice strain [@10.1002/adma.74033], MPUA converts residual tensile strain into compression [@10.1002/solr.70435], C60-azo photoisomerizes under UV to generate compressive stress at the buried interface, retaining 92.7% after 488 h [@10.1002/adma.74220], and a DOP-BTA chelation network passivates defects while relieving tensile stress in CsPbBr3 [@10.1002/adfm.77137]. THz-TDS adds a probe, with the 1.58 THz oscillator strength in MAPbI3 scaling linearly with XPS-quantified CH3NH2 defect concentration [@10.48550/arxiv.2607.15538]. Stability claims are not directly comparable because protocols differ, though retention above 90% appears across the works cited above. A review argues that perovskite heterostructures reduce defect density, suppress ion migration, and relieve strain simultaneously [@10.1002/adma.73851], and simulated champions of 29.08% and 29.64% from SCAPS-1D studies remain computational upper bounds [@10.1016/j.hybadv.2026.100700] [@10.1002/pip.70131].
