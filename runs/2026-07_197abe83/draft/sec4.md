Of the 648 works screened this month, 119 (18.4%) address interfaces, 39 read closely, and 64 (9.9%) address device architecture, 22 read closely. This is the first month in the series, so no month-over-month comparison is possible.

The buried hole-selective contact of inverted cells is the underexplored factor controlling efficiency and stability [@10.1002/adfm.77486]. Multidentate anchoring recurs among the best results. A bisphosphonate mediator reconstructs the NiOx/SAM interface and coordinates undercoordinated Pb2+, certified 27.31% [@10.1002/adma.73807]. A tetradentate Fe(III) porphyrin co-SAM reaches 27.06% champion and 26.82% certified on 21.54 cm2 [@10.1002/anie.8080769]. The noncoplanar molecule TTA blocks pi-pi SAM aggregation, giving 33.3% champion and 33.1% certified, 23.7% in wide-bandgap junctions near 1.67 eV [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945]. A sulfonyl calixarene host-guest layer stabilizes the buried interface, 27.12% champion and certified 22.25% on 655.2 cm2 aperture [@10.1038/s41467-026-74018-8].

Co-adsorption and dipole tuning form a second cluster. Proton-transfer-complex confinement improves carbazole SAM dispersion, 26.23% and 1368 h at 75 °C [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01489]. The phosphine TFMP joins Me-4PACz and 4PABCz in a ternary SAM, bridging the layers by coordinating Pb2+ and stabilizing FA+, 26.78% [@10.1002/adma.74416]. Co-assembled 4AA with DB18C6 lifts coverage from 0.57 to 0.79 and the dipole from 2 to 7 Debye, 26.33% [@10.1002/adma.73886]. Fluorinated F-2PACz strengthens the dipole and passivates halide vacancies, 25.75% champion and 25.06% on 1 cm2 with only 3.11% relative loss [@10.1002/adfm.76878]. Aggregation control is a shared target: BMIMBF4 ionic liquid lubricates the SAM, 26.82% with 92.3% after 800 h at 85 °C [@10.1002/anie.7090517], while Lindqvist polyoxometalates raise anchoring from 29.93% to 53.98% and cut desorption from 30.7% to 5.6%, 25.15% [@10.26599/pom.2026.9140147]. Tetraphosphorylated phthalocyanine SAMs reach modules, certified 23.1% and stabilized 22.9% on 20.3 cm2, no degradation over 61 days of ISOS-O-1 outdoor aging [@10.1126/sciadv.aee4175].

In n-i-p stacks the SnO2 interface dominates. A preburied 2D perovskite forms in situ at SnO2/perovskite, passivating defects and switching tensile to compressive strain, 25.67% [@10.1002/adfm.76848]. Dipotassium methanedisulfonate coordinates undercoordinated Sn4+, passivates oxygen vacancies, and induces a positive dipole, 25.76% [@10.1002/adfm.77175]. The chelators PDA and PCA bind Pb2+ via phenanthroline nitrogens, closing bottom-interface voids, 25.15% on 0.04 cm2 [@10.1002/smll.74604]. An ultrathin PTS layer anchors the perovskite/C60 contact via sulfonate and pyrene pi-pi interactions, 26.53%, 96% after 1000 h of MPPT and 91% after 300 cycles from -40 to 85 °C [@10.1021/jacs.6c06932]. At the top contact, MeO-2PACz suppresses photoinduced phase segregation and I2 expulsion [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01666].

Non-SAM hole layers see similar work. NiOx at 20% PO2 doubles extraction, defect density falling from 3.01e11 to 1.71e11 cm-3, cells still at 13.7% [@10.1002/solr.70425]. SAMs on NiOx raise fracture energy from 0.94 to 4.82 J m-2, about tenfold over ITO [@10.1021/acsmaterialslett.6c00479]. NaOAc exchange lifts the PEDOT:PSS work function from 4.27 to 4.38 eV and conductivity by 55%, 19.38% [@10.1021/acsami.6c08440]. In CsPbI3, BDA with 5-FBA lifts cells from 12.93% to 20.71% at fill factors to 83.92% [@10.1002/smll.74356].

SCAPS-1D names FTO/ZnO/BaSnO3/(FAPbI3)0.85(MAPbBr3)0.15/Au the best of seven HTL-free designs, 29.1% [@10.1016/j.hybadv.2026.100721], and a Pt/Cu2BaSnS4 stack peaks at 30.81% at 300 K, falling with temperature [@10.1002/ente.70590]. A surrogate model puts a tolerant CsPbBr3/CsPbI3/MASnI3 tandem at 29.61% versus 28.86% nominal [@10.1002/solr.70434].

The architecture axis converges on two-terminal tandems, beyond the roughly 29.4% crystalline-silicon limit that laboratory devices have crossed [@10.1021/acs.chemrev.5c01014]. Sr2+ stiffens the metal-halide framework and weakens electron-phonon coupling, 31.66% on fully-textured perovskite/silicon tandems [@10.1002/aenm.71317]. TPB-Br yields 23.63% in the 1.68 eV sub-cell and 32.03% on 1.05 cm2, retaining 90.1%, 81.2%, and 93.4% after 1200 h of storage, thermal aging, and light soaking [@10.1002/anie.5530609]. A radiation-hardened tandem keeps certified 27.49% on 12.56 cm2, nearly 80% after 1 MeV electrons at 1×10^14 e/cm2 and 93% after 150 keV protons at 1×10^12 p/cm2 [@10.1126/sciadv.aef6600], though AM0 MPPT between -100 and +100 °C shows wide-gap segregation above 50 °C, only partly reversed in 45-min dark cooling [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c00992]. Co-evaporated perovskite/CIGS reaches 26.4% champion and 25.9% certified, rigid 26.01% with Voc 1.85 V without NiOx [@10.1002/aenm.71267], and NPAI-modified four-terminal semitransparent cells give a 27.7% stack with 21.4% cells, 61.3% average NIR transmittance over 800-1200 nm, and 94.5% after 2000 bends at 10 mm radius [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01361].

All-perovskite tandems advance through crystallization control. A solvent-resistant 3-TMA interlayer supports 33.21% champion and 32.13% certified, with 23.17% and 19.61% sub-cells at 1.67 and 1.84 eV [@10.1002/anie.1676645]. Controlled MA incorporation suppresses Sn-Pb segregation and Sn2+ oxidation, 30.13% champion and 29.57% certified, a 23.90% certified Sn-Pb junction [@10.1002/adma.74078]. SALC coordination with SnI2 homogenizes Sn/Pb, 29.44% champion and 28.84% certified tandems with 22.99% singles at 0.892 V [@10.1126/sciadv.aeb8790]. A language model screened over 8000 solvents for a 32.5% tandem, picking gamma-valerolactone, which coordinates FA+ and orients grain growth [@10.1007/s40820-026-02291-9]. Dry-contact trimming removes Sn-rich species without a retained 2D cap, lifting Sn-Pb cells above 24% toward a 30% tandem [@10.1002/aenm.202506753], a graphene oxide/SAM bilayer gives 27.3% champion and 27.0% stabilized tandems from 22.1% and 0.88 V single cells holding 90% after 770 h at 25 °C [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102575], and zoledronic acid anchoring supports a 30.62% tandem whose 20.55% sub-cell keeps over 90% after 200 h of MPP [@10.1002/smll.74999].

Tin chemistry closes the axis. Zero-valent Sn nanoparticles eliminate Sn4+ by redox shuttling, certified 23.26% on 4.05 mm2, over 90% after 500 h of MPP [@10.1016/j.nanoen.2026.112222], while MnCl2 and ZnCl2 expel tin as volatile SnCl4, leaving grain-boundary passivation, 23.06% [@10.1021/acsnano.5c21750]. Where SnF2 and reductants fail to tame crystallization in thick inverted absorbers, the (111)-orienting 4F-PEABr gives 21.5% on 0.128 cm2 with the best MPP stability [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c00858]. Ionic-liquid/polyurethane grain-boundary bridges suppress halide segregation, 20.85% wide-gap cells with T90 beyond 1000 h in a 25.92% stack [@10.1002/adma.74183].
