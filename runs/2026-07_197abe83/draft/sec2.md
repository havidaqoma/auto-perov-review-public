The verified performance frontier reaches a power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 33.1% in two-terminal (2T) tandem architectures and 27.31% in single-junction inverted (p-i-n) devices, yet only 22 of 169 closely read papers reported an independently certified efficiency. In 2T tandems, certified values of 33.1% and 32.13%, along with intermediate benchmarks at 29.57%, 28.84%, and 27.49%, confirm that current matching across paired subcells dictates the verifiable ceiling [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945] [@10.1002/anie.1676645] [@10.1002/adma.74078] [@10.1126/sciadv.aeb8790] [@10.1126/sciadv.aef6600]. Across p-i-n single junctions, certified PCE concentrates tightly at 27.31% and 27.3%, establishing an interfacial recombination barrier where non-radiative losses at transport layers suppress open-circuit voltage [@10.1002/adma.73807] [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456] [@10.1126/science.aed8175].

Area expansion reveals how uncertified laboratory reports outpace verified metrics. For a 625 cm2 aperture, self-reported efficiency reached 27.8% despite an independently certified baseline of 27.3% [@10.1126/science.aed8175]. Certified large-area devices quantify the resistive and geometric penalties imposed by macroscopic transport: 818 cm2 achieved 26.53% [@10.1021/jacs.6c06932], 766 cm2 sustained 27.31% [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456], 684.75 cm2 reached 27.31% [@10.1002/adma.73807], and 655.2 cm2 delivered 27.12% [@10.1038/s41467-026-74018-8]. These metrics verify that scalable interconnects and spatial uniformity can suppress series resistance across large modules.

A champion efficiency obtained from an unshadowed, sub-0.1 cm2 laboratory cell and an independently certified measurement across a module represent irreconcilable categories of physical evidence. Microscale test structures probe localized carrier generation under negligible series resistance, systematically masking pinhole shunting, film non-uniformity, and perimeter recombination. Conversely, certified module-scale metrics integrate transparent electrode sheet resistance, scribe dead zones, and macroscopic phase segregation across active layers. Equating isolated laboratory spikes with certified module performance mistakes local material potential for verified device technology.
