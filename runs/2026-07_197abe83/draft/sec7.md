Monolithic perovskite-silicon tandems have pushed power conversion efficiency (PCE) to 33.21% (certified 32.13%) and 32.5% through interface and crystallization engineering [@10.1002/anie.1676645] [@10.1007/s40820-026-02291-9], but reported performance exceeds what operating stability currently licenses. Two physical impediments dominate: interfacial recombination at the hole-selective contact and halide phase segregation in wide-bandgap (WBG) absorbers.

At the buried contact, self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) suffer from solvent-induced degradation and inhomogeneous nucleation. Inserting a 3-TMA molecular interlayer between the SAM and the absorber regulates crystallization via hydrogen bonding, reaching 23.17% PCE in 1.67 eV and 19.61% in 1.84 eV single-junction devices [@10.1002/anie.1676645], while combining graphene oxide with SAMs forms a contact that retains 90% of initial efficiency after 770 h at 25 °C in a 27.3% tandem [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102575]. Coordinating formamidinium cations with gamma-valerolactone (GVL) cosolvents similarly retards nucleation to enforce oriented micrometer-scale grain growth across the buried interface [@10.1007/s40820-026-02291-9] [@10.1002/advs.76463].

Within the bulk, mixed-halide absorbers undergo light- and heat-induced phase segregation. Under in-operando thermal cycling from -100 to +100 °C, WBG subcells exhibit severe phase segregation above 50 °C with only partial recovery during dark cooling [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c00992]. Halogen homogenization via TPB-Br surface passivation [@10.1002/anie.5530609] and conductive polymer grain-boundary bridges [@10.1002/adma.74183] suppress halide demixing, yielding 23.63% (1.68 eV subcell) and 20.85% single junctions alongside uncertified tandems of 32.03% on 1.05 cm2 and 25.92% [@10.1002/anie.5530609] [@10.1002/adma.74183]. Strengthening the metal-halide octahedral framework via Sr2+ alloying weakens electron-phonon coupling, achieving 31.66% PCE with an operational lifetime to 80% of initial efficiency (T80) of 6785 h on fully-textured silicon [@10.1002/aenm.71317].

Upscaling these stacks exposes severe area-dependent transport penalties. Quantitative hyperspectral luminescence imaging on 64 cm2 modules reveals that resistive transport and interconnection losses equal intrinsic material losses [@10.1002/solr.70375]. Scalable printing using low-toxicity GVL with dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) exceeds 17% in WBG cells and 28% in tandems [@10.1002/advs.76463], while ionic liquid stabilization demonstrates certified 27.49% tandems on 12.56 cm2 with 23.58% certified top-cell efficiency [@10.1126/sciadv.aef6600].
