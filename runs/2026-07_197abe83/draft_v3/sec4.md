Non-radiative recombination at carrier-selective contacts in perovskite solar cells (PSCs) is driven by undercoordinated lead(II) (Pb2+) cations, dangling bonds, and halide vacancies that require Lewis acid-base coordination [@10.1002/anie.1864800] [@10.1002/aenm.71305] [@10.1002/adma.73950] [@10.1002/adfm.77378] [@10.20517/energymater.2026.102]. At hole-transport layer (HTL) contacts, carbazole-based self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) coordinate Pb2+ to reach 26.85% power conversion efficiency (PCE) [@10.1002/anie.1864800], while carboxyl-sulfur molecules coordinate dangling bonds and free lead(II) iodide (PbI2) for an uncertified 26.7% PCE [@10.1002/adma.73950]. At electron-transport layer (ETL) contacts, 1-(4'-sulfophenyl)-3-carboxyl-5-pyrazolone sodium salt (SCP) yields a certified 26.84% PCE (champion 27.25%, stabilised 26.84%) with 90% retention after 1485 h of maximum power point tracking (MPPT) [@10.1002/aenm.71305]. Amphoteric ionic liquids passivate both acidic and basic sites for 26.13% champion PCE (20.06% PCE and 85.74% fill factor (FF) in 1.84 eV cells) [@10.1002/adfm.77378], while Pb-N coordination and vacancy filling preserves 88.67% efficiency after 1000 h MPPT at 65 °C (uncertified 25.06% PCE) [@10.20517/energymater.2026.102]. Passivating tin(IV) oxide (SnO2) oxygen vacancies with conjugated sorbate anions yields an uncertified 22.16% PCE (open-circuit voltage (Voc) 1.183 V, FF 84.30%) [@10.1007/s42114-026-01958-6]. Plasma surface engineering passivates laser scribes, securing a certified 23.5% PCE on a 65.05 cm2 module and 27.2% small-cell champion PCE (98.1% retained after 2000 h MPPT at 85 °C) [@10.1126/science.aeg1730].

Electric-field- and heat-induced migration of halides and A-site cations accelerates phase segregation, requiring chemical trapping and dimensional barriers [@10.1002/anie.2924886] [@10.1126/sciadv.aed6327] [@10.1002/adfm.77015] [@10.1002/ece2.70102]. Dual iodide-trapping molecules immobilize mobile halides at the HTL interface for 26.81% champion PCE and a T93 lifetime over 1140 h at 85 °C [@10.1002/anie.2924886], while acetylthiocholine chloride (ATC) coordinates Pb2+ and fills vacancies to suppress segregation, yielding 22.68% champion PCE on a 63.7 cm2 module [@10.1002/ece2.70102]. Two-dimensional/three-dimensional (2D/3D) heterojunctions formed by halide imidazolium cations block ion pathways, retaining 92% efficiency after 1200 h at 85 °C (26.33% champion PCE) [@10.1002/adfm.77015]. In situ two-dimensional (2D) capping via hydrogen bonds with formamidinium (FA+) retains 90% efficiency after 800 h at 85 °C in N2, yet evaluation on a 0.072 cm2 aperture (26.32% champion PCE) leaves scalability unproven [@10.1002/advs.76508]. Molten salt dissolution of 2D phases suppresses random nucleation and eliminates interfacial PbI2, boosting PCE from 23.43% to 25.73% [@10.1002/adfm.76974]. Unified ligand-dimensional designs halt cation migration, achieving certified 26.52% PCE (26.88% champion) on small cells and certified 17.75% PCE (20.25% uncertified) on 900 cm2 modules, retaining over 91% efficiency after 1000 h at 85 °C and 85% relative humidity (RH) [@10.1126/sciadv.aed6327].

Residual tensile strain accelerates defect nucleation and ion transport, coupling mechanical stress to electronic loss [@10.1002/adma.74033] [@10.1002/adfm.77373] [@10.1002/adma.74220] [@10.1038/s41467-026-75629-x] [@10.1002/aenm.71312]. Coordinating adjacent Pb2+ with dithiopyr relieves lattice strain and directs preferential (100) orientation, yielding certified 26.14% PCE (26.9% champion) and 21.28% on 30 × 30 cm2 modules [@10.1002/adma.74033]. Cross-linked 2-amino-3-hydroxybutanoic acid (AHBA) networks relax buried SnO2 interface strain and accelerate electron extraction to 3.5 ps for 26.32% champion PCE [@10.1002/adfm.77373]. Low-temperature liquid-assisted vibrational stress-relief (LTL-VSR) relieves macroscopic stress without additives, yielding 26.47% rigid and 24.64% flexible cell efficiencies, alongside 23.59% (16.8 cm2) and 20.55% (600 cm2) module efficiencies [@10.1038/s41467-026-75629-x]. Photoresponsive fullerene C60-azo generates compressive stress under ultraviolet (UV) light via trans-to-cis isomerization, retaining 92.7% performance after 488 h UV exposure with 26.6% champion PCE [@10.1002/adma.74220]. Finally, phenethylammonium iodide (PEAI) paired with cerium oxide (CeOx) provides redox-buffering passivation, retaining 95% efficiency after 100 thermal shock cycles between 80 °C and -80 °C (at 16 °C/min) with 25.3% champion PCE in poly(triarylamine) (PTAA)-based devices [@10.1002/aenm.71312].
