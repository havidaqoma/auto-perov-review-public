The area penalty during module scale-up stems from three coupled physical bottlenecks: series-resistance losses across monolithic interconnects, non-uniform crystallization in scalable coating, and localized interfacial recombination [@10.1002/solr.70375] [@10.1002/adma.74316] [@10.1126/science.aed8175]. Extrinsic integration and resistive interconnect losses rival intrinsic material recombination in driving the efficiency drop from discrete cells to series-connected modules [@10.1002/solr.70375] [@10.1002/adma.74316]. Hyperspectral luminescence imaging attributes this deficit to resistive transport across interconnections in 64 cm2 modules [@10.1002/solr.70375], while electrostatic templating with indol-3-ylethylammonium iodide spacer cations leaves a certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) gap between 26.94% on small-area cells and 23.21% on a 25 cm2 monolithic module [@10.1002/adma.74316]. Aperture scaling demonstrates that modules can maintain high efficiencies: a certified 27.3% PCE (champion 27.8%) across 625 cm2 with nonfullerene electron-transport layers (ETLs) [@10.1126/science.aed8175], alongside uncertified champion outputs of 27.12% on 655.2 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-026-74018-8], 27.31% on 684.75 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.73807], 27.31% on 766 cm2 [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456], and 26.53% on 818 cm2 [@10.1021/jacs.6c06932].

Transitioning from spin-coating to scalable meniscus blade coating or vapor methods induces drying gradients and phase degradation [@10.1002/aenm.71336] [@10.1038/s41467-026-75053-1] [@10.1021/acsami.6c07418] [@10.1002/adfm.77211]. Incorporating Lewis base and zwitterion dual additives into blade-coated tin-lead perovskite solar cells (PSCs) yields grains over 800 nm and elevates open-circuit voltage (Voc) from 0.797 to 0.850 V, achieving 21.02% single-junction PCE and a two-terminal (2T) tandem champion PCE of 27.84% (27.33% stabilised) [@10.1002/aenm.71336]. For ambient fabrication, surface-confined protection stabilizes pre-annealing crystallization, enabling air-processed blade-coated methylammonium (MA)-free PSCs to achieve 26.14% champion and 25.75% certified PCE, with 93.11% retention after 1300 h of continuous 1-sun maximum power point tracking (MPPT) [@10.1038/s41467-026-75053-1]. Vapor-phase annealing (VPA) establishes uniform thermal-humidity fields to direct bottom-up crystallization, yielding an additive-free champion PCE of 22.15% [@10.1021/acsami.6c07418], whereas stabilizing precursor solutions against aging via anion-pi interactions preserves 25.76% efficiency after 7 days and 23.88% after 3 months [@10.1002/adfm.77211]. High-throughput quantification of microstructural homogeneity is assisted by deep segmentation frameworks such as PerovSegNet, which reaches 87.25% mean average precision (mAP) in scanning electron microscopy (SEM) image analysis at 25.22% lower computational cost [@10.1038/s41524-026-02211-w]. Replacing fullerene ETLs with the nonfullerene conjugated polymer 2PB-T also addresses contact degradation, retaining over 98.6% of initial PCE after 1752 h of continuous MPPT at 85 °C in air on small-area cells [@10.1126/science.aed8175].
