The buried interface between the hole-transport layer (HTL) and perovskite absorber dictates carrier extraction kinetics and mechanical reliability in inverted perovskite solar cells (PSCs) [@10.1002/adfm.77486]. Weak chemisorption and disordered self-assembled monolayer (SAM) packing on metal oxides encourage molecular desorption and interfacial delamination; removing loosely bound molecules increases the interfacial fracture energy from 0.94 to 4.82 J m-2 [@10.1021/acsmaterialslett.6c00479]. To prevent interfacial detachment, multidentate chelating architectures substitute weak monodentate anchoring [@10.1002/anie.8080769] [@10.1002/adma.73807] [@10.1002/smll.74999]. Tetraphosphonic acid iron(III) porphyrins establish tetradentate chelation to oxide substrates, yielding a certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 26.82% (27.06% champion) [@10.1002/anie.8080769]. Bisphosphonate head groups similarly reconstruct nickel oxide (NiOx) interfaces by coordinating undercoordinated lead (Pb2+) to achieve a certified 27.31% PCE [@10.1002/adma.73807], or reinforce anchoring via hydrogen bonding to retain over 90% of initial performance after 200 h of maximum power point tracking (MPPT) [@10.1002/smll.74999]. Tetraphosphorylated phthalocyanines lock interfacial packing to secure a certified 23.1% PCE (22.9% stabilised) on a 20.3 cm2 module with zero degradation across 61 days of outdoor testing under International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability (ISOS) ISOS-O-1 protocols [@10.1126/sciadv.aee4175]. Beyond anchoring strength, self-aggregation via pi-pi stacking creates pinholes that expose bare transparent conductive oxides. Introducing noncoplanar geometry suppresses aggregation to achieve a certified 33.1% tandem PCE (33.3% champion; 23.7% single-junction) [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945]. Alternatively, co-adsorbing Lindqvist polyoxometalates increases the SAM anchoring ratio from 29.93% to 53.98% while reducing the desorption rate from 30.7% to 5.6% during continuous MPPT [@10.26599/pom.2026.9140147]. Interfacial aggregation is also counteracted using ionic liquid lubricants that preserve 92.3% initial efficiency after 800 h at 85 °C under air mass 1.5 global (AM 1.5G) illumination [@10.1002/anie.7090517], proton-transfer complexes resisting 75 °C degradation for 1368 h [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01489], or thermally activated in situ polymerization networks that achieve an uncertified 26.87% PCE [@10.1002/anie.1018855].

At the absorber contact, asymmetric nucleation generates buried microvoids, tensile lattice strain, and deep defect states that induce non-radiative recombination [@10.1002/adfm.76848] [@10.1002/smll.74604]. Bidentate phenanthroline molecules chelate Pb2+ and facilitate dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) solvent evaporation to eliminate interfacial voids, delivering a 25.15% PCE, but this evidence is constrained to an uncertified 0.04 cm2 aperture area [@10.1002/smll.74604]. Supramolecular calixarenes mitigate defect propagation via host-guest interactions, yielding a certified 27.12% cell PCE alongside a certified 22.25% on a 655.2 cm2 module [@10.1038/s41467-026-74018-8]. Electrostatic alignment and defect passivation proceed concurrently through molecular dipoles: sulfonate-anchored dipotassium salts coordinate undercoordinated tin (Sn4+) sites on tin oxide (SnO2) electron-transport layers (ETLs) to passivate oxygen vacancies [@10.1002/adfm.77175], while fluorinated carbazole SAMs passivate halide vacancies, scaling to 1 cm2 with 25.06% PCE and 3.11% relative loss [@10.1002/adfm.76878]. Polycyclic aromatic SAMs with tailored electrostatic potentials (26.20% PCE) [@10.1002/anie.4031162], multifunctional modifiers (26.67% PCE) [@10.1002/aenm.71282], ternary phosphine bridges stabilizing formamidinium cations [@10.1002/adma.74416], and preburied two-dimensional (2D) perovskite inducers that invert tensile to compressive strain [@10.1002/adfm.76848] similarly regulate the heterojunction. Nevertheless, theoretical one-dimensional Solar Cell Capacitance Simulator (SCAPS-1D) simulations predict ideal efficiencies exceeding 30%, including 30.81% at 300 K [@10.1002/ente.70590] and 29.1% in HTL-free bilayer ETLs [@10.1016/j.hybadv.2026.100721], yet these drift-diffusion calculations omit the spatial heterogeneity of SAM desorption, localized pinholes, and void formation that dominate physical devices.
