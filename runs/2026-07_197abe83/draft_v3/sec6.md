Depth-resolved profiling reveals that operational photo-degradation initiates at both buried and top interfaces before propagating into bulk perovskite absorbers [@10.1002/solr.70421]. At buried hole-transport layer (HTL) contacts in inverted perovskite solar cells (PSCs), molecular disorder accelerates non-radiative recombination. Rigidifying molecular orientation by coassembling carbazole self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) suppresses interfacial defects, yielding a certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 27.31% for single cells and 21.54% for a 766 cm2 module under International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability (ISOS) protocol ISOS-L-1 [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456]. Tailoring asymmetric benzothienoindole SAMs with electron-withdrawing substituents similarly improves packing and energetics, delivering 25.38% on 1 cm2 and 20.59% on a 655.2 cm2 module [@10.1002/anie.1678852]. At the top contact, multidentate fullerenes anchor phenethylammonium bromide capping layers against thermal loss, retaining 98.7% initial PCE after 2100 h of maximum power point tracking (MPPT) under continuous illumination and 95.8% after 1800 h at 85 °C under ISOS-L-3 [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01517], while phase-pure two-dimensional (2D) perovskite reconstruction passivates surfaces [@10.1002/adma.74247] [@10.1002/solr.70447]. Extrinsic ion migration also accelerates failure: lithium bis(trifluoromethanesulfonyl)imide (Li-TFSI) dopants in HTLs release mobile lithium ions that drift under bias, whereas volatile dopants eliminate ionic residues [@10.1002/smll.74289]. Surface passivation via aromatic carbonyl molecules yields 26.12% PCE, but only on an uncertified, sub-0.1 cm2 area (0.09 cm2) [@10.1002/anie.5216345].

In wide-bandgap (WBG) mixed-halide absorbers, operational instability stems from electric-field-induced halide segregation driven by trapped-charge accumulation [@10.1002/aenm.71358]. Operating cells under maximum stability point tracking (MSPT) instead of MPPT alleviates trapped carriers, retaining approximately 90% initial power after 700 h [@10.1002/aenm.71358]. Chemical stabilization targets the same thermodynamic vulnerability: incorporating 1,4-phenylenediamine dihydriodide into 1.93-eV absorbers raises the ion migration activation barrier to suppress phase segregation, achieving a certified PCE of 26.18% with an open-circuit voltage (Voc) of 3.148 V on 1.17 cm2 triple-junction cells [@10.1038/s41467-026-75305-0]. Rubidium incorporation similarly homogenizes vertical cesium distribution in 1.67-eV pure-iodide perovskites to prevent demixing, reaching an uncertified 22.51% PCE under ISOS-L-1 [@10.1021/acsnano.6c04084].

Under thermal and light fluctuations, dynamic lattice strain drives degradation [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01837]: residual tensile stress relaxes via diffusion-mediated inelastic deformation, accelerating moisture uptake by an order of magnitude [@10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-128tn/v2]. Interfacial networks and printed silicon oxynitride barrier films counter moisture penetration, the latter retaining 93% ± 5% PCE after 1000 h at 25 °C and 60% relative humidity (RH) under ISOS-D-1 [@10.1002/smll.74816] [@10.1007/s40820-026-02295-5] [@10.1126/science.aef1969]. Yet a methodological gap obscures durability: only 4 of 169 closely read studies reported the time to 80% initial efficiency (T80), spanning 200 h, 720 h, 6785 h, and 9800 h [@10.1002/smll.74531] [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01637] [@10.1002/aenm.71317] [@10.1002/adma.74243], and only 6 specified an ISOS protocol [@10.1126/sciadv.aee4175] [@10.1021/acsnano.6c04084] [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01517] [@10.1021/jacs.6c06932] [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456] [@10.26599/pom.2026.9140147]. Prevailing tests apply constant 1-sun illumination at mild temperatures, probing photocarrier collection while failing to evaluate thermomechanical fatigue, reverse bias, or dark-light cycles.
