Monolithic tandems can surpass the ~29.4% crystalline silicon (c-Si) efficiency limit [@10.1021/acs.chemrev.5c01014], yet two-terminal (2T) integration remains constrained by halide phase segregation in wide-bandgap (WBG) top absorbers and interfacial non-radiative recombination. In-operando thermal cycling between -100 and +100 °C under air mass 0 (AM0) illumination reveals that WBG subcells segregate rapidly above 50 °C with only partial recovery during dark cooling, accelerating non-radiative voltage loss [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c00992]. Suppressing this demixing requires reinforcing octahedral framework integrity and passivating grain boundaries [@10.1002/aenm.71317] [@10.1002/anie.5530609] [@10.1002/adma.74183]. B-site strontium (Sr2+) alloying strengthens metal-halide bonding and weakens electron-phonon coupling, yielding uncertified 31.66% power conversion efficiency (PCE) in fully-textured perovskite/silicon tandems with an operational T80 lifetime (time to 80% initial efficiency) of 6,785 hours [@10.1002/aenm.71317]. At grain boundaries, TPB-Br molecular extrusion drives halogen homogenization to deliver 23.63% PCE in 1.68 electronvolt (eV) subcells and an uncertified 32.03% on 1.05 cm2 tandems [@10.1002/anie.5530609], while conductive ionic-liquid/polyurethane polymer bridges passivate defect states to yield uncertified 20.85% WBG and 25.92% flexible tandem PCEs [@10.1002/adma.74183]. Ionic-liquid additives similarly confer radiation resilience, yielding certified 27.49% monolithic tandems over 12.56 cm2 that maintain 93% efficiency under proton irradiation [@10.1126/sciadv.aef6600].

Defective buried contacts and unguided nucleation introduce large open-circuit voltage (Voc) deficits. Retarding nucleation via coordinating solvents directs micrometer-scale grain growth [@10.1007/s40820-026-02291-9] [@10.1002/advs.76463]: gamma-valerolactone (GVL) coordination with formamidinium (FA+) cations achieves uncertified 32.5% champion tandem PCEs [@10.1007/s40820-026-02291-9], while GVL-dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) inks yield printed cells exceeding 17% and 28% tandems [@10.1002/advs.76463]. Regulating crystallization and passivating defects at hole-transport layers (HTLs) further limits interface recombination [@10.1002/anie.1676645] [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102575] [@10.1002/aenm.71267]. Inserting a 3-TMA interlayer on self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) regulates crystallization through hydrogen bonding, achieving 23.17% (1.67 eV) and 19.61% (1.84 eV) subcells and a certified 32.13% (33.21% champion) silicon tandem [@10.1002/anie.1676645]. Bilayer graphene oxide/SAM HTLs similarly stabilize triple-junction devices at 27.0% stabilized PCE (27.3% champion) [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102575], whereas omitting nickel oxide (NiOx) HTLs via hybrid deposition yields flexible perovskite/copper indium gallium selenide (CIGS) tandems with certified 25.9% PCE (26.4% champion, 1.85 V Voc) [@10.1002/aenm.71267].
