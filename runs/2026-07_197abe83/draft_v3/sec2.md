Only 22 of 169 closely read papers reported an independently certified power conversion efficiency (PCE), revealing that most literature relies on unverified laboratory metrics. Certified performance ceilings diverge across device architectures. For perovskite/silicon tandems, verified efficiency reached 33.1% in an inverted p-type-intrinsic-n-type (p-i-n) structure [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945] alongside 32.13% in two-terminal (2T) devices [@10.1002/anie.1676645]. In all-perovskite 2T tandems, narrow-bandgap subcell limitations and non-radiative recombination constrained the certified ceiling to 29.57% [@10.1002/adma.74078], with lower certified tiers at 28.84% [@10.1126/sciadv.aeb8790] and 27.49% [@10.1126/sciadv.aef6600]. Single-junction p-i-n perovskite solar cells (PSCs) converged between 27.3% and 27.31% [@10.1002/adma.73807] [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456] [@10.1126/science.aed8175].

Device area exposes a fundamental evidentiary divide: a self-reported champion on a sub-0.1 cm2 cell and a certified value on a module represent distinct classes of physical evidence. Uncertified champion micro-cells suppress lateral series resistance and avoid macroscopic defects, allowing self-reported efficiencies to run ahead of certified limits. Conversely, large-area modules must overcome interconnect resistance, transparent conductor sheet resistance, and non-uniform film crystallization. Demonstrating scalable transport, certified p-i-n modules verified 27.31% across both 766 cm2 [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456] and 684.75 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.73807].

Beyond these verified benchmarks, uncertified claims overstate large-area performance. Uncertified reports claimed 27.12% on 655.2 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-026-74018-8] and 26.53% on 818 cm2 [@10.1021/jacs.6c06932], while self-reported in-lab testing on a 625 cm2 module claimed 27.8% despite achieving an independent certification of only 27.3% [@10.1126/science.aed8175]. This discrepancy highlights how uncertified testing masks transient capacitive currents and aperture misalignment, confirming that unverified claims on sub-0.1 cm2 cells distort the true technological frontier.
