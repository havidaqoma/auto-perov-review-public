The quantitative frontier for perovskite photovoltaics is defined by single-junction power conversion efficiency (PCE) reaching certified values of 26.52% (26.88% champion) [@10.1126/sciadv.aed6327] and uncertified reports exceeding 26% [@10.1002/advs.76508] [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15006632/v1]. Whether these metrics reflect phase-pure lattices or metastable polymorphic mixtures remains governed by precursor kinetics, phase segregation, and spatial scalability. Record efficiencies frequently rely on sub-0.1 cm2 apertures, such as 26.32% on 0.072 cm2 [@10.1002/advs.76508], whereas scaling to a 900 cm2 module lowers efficiency to 20.25% (17.75% certified) despite retaining over 91% initial efficiency after 1000 hours at 85°C and 85% relative humidity (RH) [@10.1126/sciadv.aed6327].

Controlling precursor coordination prevents solvent degradation and defective phase precipitation [@10.1021/jacs.6c05502] [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01764] [@10.1002/adfm.76731]. In formamidinium (FA) lead triiodide (FAPbI3) growth, parasitic esterification between gamma-butyrolactone (GBL) and 2-methoxyethanol (2ME) destabilizes heated solutions; maintaining lead iodide (PbI2) deficiency delays redissolution to suppress delta-phase impurities, yielding 22.99% PCE [@10.1021/jacs.6c05502]. Additives also eliminate antisolvent quenching: sulfolane forms an intermediate phase yielding 22.2% PCE in methylammonium (MA) lead triiodide (MAPbI3) [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01764], while 3-furoic acid (3-FA) coordinates tin iodide (SnI2) and hydrogen-bonds with formamidinium iodide (FAI) to decouple nucleation from crystal growth, reaching 16.16% PCE [@10.1002/adfm.76731]. Stoichiometric imbalance leaves defective binary halides: PbX2-terminated surfaces display higher defect densities and lower stability than AX terminations [@10.1021/acsnano.6c08922]. Passivating residual PbI2 with triphenyl phosphoramide (TPPA) yields 21.4% PCE and retains 90% initial efficiency after 750 hours of maximum power point tracking (MPPT) versus 270 hours for controls [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01370]. Similarly, molten 4-(4,6-dimethoxy-1,3,5-triazin-2-yl)-4-methylmorpholinium chloride (DMTMM) dissolves two-dimensional (2D) phases to eliminate interfacial PbI2, raising PCE from 23.43% to 25.73% [@10.1002/adfm.76974], while optimizing PbI2 evaporation distance achieves 14.57% PCE over 64 cm2 [@10.1002/solr.70430].

Beyond nucleation, compositional heterogeneity drives phase demixing [@10.1002/adma.74382] [@10.1016/j.solmat.2026.114597]. Concurrent four-dimensional scanning transmission electron microscopy (4D-STEM) and energy-dispersive X-ray spectroscopy (EDX) reveals that photoinactive hexagonal polytypes nucleate from nanoscale cesium (Cs) deficiencies [@10.1002/adma.74382]. In mixed-halide absorbers, strontium doping prevents I2Br- intermediate formation, suppressing segregation to reach 20.18% PCE at 1.77 eV and 19.02% at 1.84 eV [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01113]. Rubidium A-site incorporation induces lattice distortion to suppress halide demixing, providing an open-circuit voltage (Voc) of 1.46 V in wide-bandgap sub-cells and 3.31 V in triple-junction tandems [@10.1002/adfm.76715]. Conversely, trivalent bismuth substitution in cesium lead halides (CsPbX3) triggers phase segregation due to lower thermodynamic stability [@10.1016/j.solmat.2026.114597]. Dielectric-response screening of 5043 compositions confirms that phase stability requires narrow windows of 2–8% Cs, 78–89% FA, 3–18% MA, and 93–99% iodide (I) [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15005541/v1].

Dimensional heterostructuring stabilizes interfaces, but phase purity remains constrained by transport penalties and area scaling [@10.1002/advs.76508] [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15006632/v1] [@10.1002/anie.7827890]. In situ hydrogen-bonded hydroxyphenethylammonium (HPA) spacer networks retain 95.5% of initial efficiency after 3000 hours at 30°C in N2, 92.5% in 45% ± 5% RH air, and 90% after 800 hours at 85°C in N2, but on 0.072 cm2 cells at 26.32% PCE [@10.1002/advs.76508]. Colloidal suspension epitaxy yields deterministic 26.0% multilayers without disturbing underlying layers [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15006632/v1]. In flexible tin cells, interfacial hydrogen bonding yields 15.56% PCE (14.67% certified) on 0.049 cm2, but drops to 11.06% at 1 cm2 despite achieving 22.21% under 1000 lux indoor illumination [@10.1002/anie.7827890]. While precursor coordination and dimensional stabilization preserve microscopic phase purity, sub-0.1 cm2 testbeds obscure severe interfacial penalties encountered during physical scaling.
