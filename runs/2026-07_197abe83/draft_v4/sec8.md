The verified performance ceiling reaches a certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 33.1% in two-terminal (2T) tandems [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945] and 27.31% in single junctions [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456] [@10.1002/adma.73807], yet the certified reporting share was 5.9% in 2026-06 and 8.3% in 2026-07, with only 22 of 169 closely read papers reporting any certified value. These peak metrics do not license claims of operating durability, as uncertified microscale sweeps mask interfacial degradation, phase instability, and scaling penalties.

First, evidence for contact stabilization fails to establish whether interfacial dipoles and multidentate coordination withstand thermo-mechanical fatigue. While modifying transparent oxides with self-assembled monolayer (SAM) contacts and multidentate chelators elevates initial open-circuit voltage (Voc), thermal expansion mismatches induce cyclic shear stresses that trigger delamination [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945] [@10.1002/adma.73807] [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01489]. Resolving whether coordination bonds survive operating fields requires operando double-cantilever beam fracture testing paired with bias-dependent Kelvin probe force microscopy during thermal cycling.

Second, reported chemical treatments do not demonstrate permanent suppression of halide segregation in wide-bandgap (WBG) absorbers. Although molecular interlayers and B-site alloying achieve high initial efficiency in tandems [@10.1002/anie.1676645] [@10.1002/aenm.71317], in situ thermal cycling reveals phase segregation above 50 °C with only partial recovery upon cooling [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c00992]. Differentiating true thermodynamic phase stability from kinetic demixing arrest demands operando nanoscale photoluminescence spectroscopy synchronized with diffraction profiling under continuous electrical bias and elevated temperature.

Third, unstandardized testing leaves the boundary between reversible ionic polarization and irreversible decomposition unquantified. Only 4 papers reported a time to 80% of initial performance (T80) lifetime, the longest 9800 h [@10.1002/adma.74243], and only 6 papers named an International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability (ISOS) protocol [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01517] [@10.1002/aenm.71358]. Establishing ion accumulation rates under operating conditions requires operando intensity-modulated photovoltage spectroscopy coupled with secondary-ion mass spectrometry during maximum power point tracking (MPPT).

Fourth, microscale defect passivation fails to establish scalability across large apertures. Only 27 of 648 works addressed scale-up, and the largest reported aperture reached 818 cm2 [@10.1021/jacs.6c06932]. Passivators that eliminate traps on sub-0.1 cm2 apertures aggregate during meniscus drying, intensifying non-radiative recombination across laser-scribed modules [@10.1126/sciadv.aed6327] [@10.1002/solr.70375]. Spatially resolved electroluminescence imaging calibrated against local series-resistance maps during coating would isolate interconnect losses. Cross-study evaluation of interfacial stabilization failed because studies reported initial unmasked voltages rather than quantifying interfacial bonding enthalpies alongside continuous operational fill-factor retention under bias.
