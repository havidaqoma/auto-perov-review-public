The quantitative frontier for perovskite solar cells now reaches certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) values of 26.84% (champion 27.25%) in inverted devices and 23.5% on 65.05 cm2 modules (champion 27.2%), though uncertified small-area claims reach 26.85% [@10.1002/aenm.71305] [@10.1126/science.aeg1730] [@10.1002/anie.1864800]. These benchmarks force a critical evaluation of what the reported physical evidence actually licenses: whether interfacial chemical treatments permanently suppress deep trap states and mobile ion channels under operating bias, or merely provide transient electrostatic screening that degrades during continuous operation.

At perovskite interfaces, undercoordinated Pb2+ cations and halide vacancies constitute primary non-radiative recombination centers. Monodentate Lewis bases donate electron pairs from sulfur, nitrogen, or oxygen to passivate surface Pb2+ defects, as demonstrated by carbazole-based self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) such as BTP at buried interfaces, thiophene sulfur coordination, and carboxyl/sulfur bifunctional TTA, which report uncertified champion PCE values of 26.85%, 21.15%, and 26.7% [@10.1002/anie.1864800] [@10.1021/acsami.6c05331] [@10.1002/adma.73950]. However, monodentate coordination offers limited thermal resistance against cooperative defect formation. To address this, amphoteric ionic liquids (GUHS and GUDHP) and FTAC simultaneously passivate Lewis acidic and basic sites while compensating iodine vacancies, enabling GUDHP to achieve 20.06% PCE with 85.74% fill factor (FF) at 1.84 eV bandgap (champion 26.13%) and allowing FTAC to retain 88.67% of initial efficiency after 1,000 h maximum-power-point tracking (MPPT) at 65 °C despite an uncertified 25.06% champion PCE [@10.1002/adfm.77378] [@10.20517/energymater.2026.102]. Multivalent spatial anchoring further impedes defect formation: three-dimensional distribution of amino groups in tetrakis(4-aminophenyl)methane (4APM), nonlayered intermediate-site anchoring ligands, and m-bis(trifluoromethyl)aniline (mTFMA) bind PbI2 or formamidinium iodide (FAI) precursors and lengthen carrier lifetimes from 529.7 to 2479.4 ns, retaining over 95% efficiency after 1000 h MPPT at 65 °C across 14 cm2 (certified 26.26%) and over 90% PCE after 2000 h at 65 °C and 90% relative humidity (RH) (uncertified champion 26.54%, projected T80 of 9800 h) [@10.1038/s41467-026-75990-x] [@10.1002/adma.74243] [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01560].

Under continuous bias, applied electric fields drive mobile iodide migration toward extraction layers, causing interfacial degradation and phase segregation. Chemical immobilization directly arrests this migration: dual iodide-trapping agents (TFIBA and FPIFA), two-dimensional/three-dimensional (2D/3D) halide imidazolium heterojunctions, and 1-(4'-sulfophenyl)-3-carboxyl-5-pyrazolone sodium salt (SCP) layers suppress spiro-OMeTAD and electron-contact degradation, achieving an uncertified 26.81% champion PCE with T93 lifetime over 1140 h at 85 °C, 92% retention after 1200 h at 85 °C (champion 26.33%), and 90% retention after 1485 h MPPT (certified 26.84%, champion 27.25%) [@10.1002/anie.2924886] [@10.1002/adfm.77015] [@10.1002/aenm.71305]. In wide-bandgap absorbers, field-driven halide separation is suppressed by guanidine phosphate (champion 21.54% at 1.78 eV, retaining 90.64% after 1400 h) and acetylthiocholine chloride (ATC) doping, which fills vacancies with quaternary ammonium and chloride ions to restrain demixing across 63.7 cm2 modules (champion 22.68%) [@10.1002/cnl2.70189] [@10.1002/ece2.70102]. Conformal plasma surface engineering similarly mitigates defect accumulation at laser-scribed interconnects to secure a certified 23.5% module PCE on 65.05 cm2 (champion 27.2%), with small-area devices retaining 98.1% of initial PCE after 2000 h MPPT at 85 °C [@10.1126/science.aeg1730].

Lattice strain fundamentally governs ion migration kinetics by lowering vacancy hopping barriers under tensile expansion. Relieving this strain through dual-site coordination with dithiopyr bridges adjacent Pb2+ sites, promoting preferential (100) crystal orientation and yielding a certified 26.14% PCE (champion 26.9%, and 21.28% on 30 × 30 cm2 modules) [@10.1002/adma.74033]. Compressive strain engineering via the polymer MPUA (champion 24.91%), cross-linked 2-amino-3-hydroxybutanoic acid (AHBA) interfaces accelerating electron extraction to 3.5 ps (champion 26.32%), and photoresponsive C60-azo trans-to-cis isomerization under ultraviolet (UV) light (92.7% retention after 488 h, champion 26.6%) counteract tensile distortion and impede defect formation [@10.1002/solr.70435] [@10.1002/adfm.77373] [@10.1002/adma.74220]. Nevertheless, most strain and passivation studies report uncertified, small-area metrics without operando tracking of mobile ion accumulation under concurrent bias, heat, and illumination, leaving long-term field immunity unsubstantiated.
