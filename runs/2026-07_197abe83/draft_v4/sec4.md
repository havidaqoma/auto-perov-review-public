The quantitative frontier for contact-engineered perovskite photovoltaics is established by monolithic tandem configurations achieving certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) values of 33.1% (champion 33.3%) [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945] and single-junction inverted architectures reaching certified 27.31% [@10.1002/adma.73807]. Translating these efficiencies into durable devices hinges on resolving three core physical problems: weak oxide anchoring, self-assembled monolayer (SAM) aggregation, and interfacial dipole misalignment.

Substrate delamination and thermal desorption originate from fragile monodentate coordination at transparent conducting oxides. Mechanical toughness is markedly substrate-dependent: depositing SAMs on a nickel oxide (NiOx) underlayer increases interfacial fracture energy tenfold relative to indium tin oxide (ITO), while removing loosely bound molecules elevates fracture energy from 0.94 to 4.82 J m-2 [@10.1021/acsmaterialslett.6c00479]. Multidentate chelating motifs directly counter this vulnerability by coordinating undercoordinated lead (Pb2+) and reinforcing substrate anchoring: tetradentate iron(III) porphyrins yield certified 26.82% PCE (champion 27.06%) [@10.1002/anie.8080769], bisphosphonates reconstruct the NiOx/SAM buried interface to reach certified 27.31% PCE on small-area rigid cells [@10.1002/adma.73807], and zoledronic acid (ZDA) head groups yield 30.62% tandem PCE with wide-bandgap sub-cells (20.55% PCE) retaining over 90% performance after 200 h of maximum power point (MPP) tracking [@10.1002/smll.74999] [@10.1002/adma.73807] [@10.1002/anie.8080769]. Lindqvist-type polyoxometalates similarly raise the anchoring ratio on ITO from 29.93% to 53.98% and cut the desorption rate from 30.7% to 5.6% during continuous MPP tracking (champion 25.15% PCE) [@10.26599/pom.2026.9140147]. Large-area module demonstrations remain scarce, though tetraphosphorylated phthalocyanines demonstrate a certified 23.1% PCE (stabilised 22.9%) on a 20.3 cm2 module without degradation after 61 days of ISOS-O-1 outdoor aging [@10.1126/sciadv.aee4175].

Intermolecular pi-pi stacking among aromatic cores drives nanoscale SAM agglomeration, generating pinholes and phase inhomogeneities. Disrupting these interactions via steric hindrance or electrostatic confinement suppresses clustering: noncoplanar multidentate contacts (TTA) impede pi-pi stacking to stabilize wide-bandgap single-junction absorbers (approximately 1.67 eV, champion PCE of 23.7%) and tandems [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945], while ionic liquid lubricants (BMIMBF4) suppress aggregation via dual steric-electrostatic modulation, retaining 92.3% of initial efficiency after 800 h at 85 °C under air mass 1.5 global (AM 1.5G) illumination (uncertified champion 26.82% PCE) [@10.1002/anie.7090517]. Concurrently, proton transfer complex-confined carbazole SAMs maintain thermal stability at 75 °C for 1368 h (champion 26.23% PCE) [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01489], and in situ thermally activated polymerization networks seal interfacial voids to achieve 26.87% champion PCE [@10.1002/anie.1018855] [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01489] [@10.1002/anie.7090517].

Unfavorable vacuum-level shifts and unpassivated defects dictate carrier extraction deficits at the absorber boundary. Halogenation and co-assembly modulate work functions: substituting methoxy groups with fluorine in F-2PACz strengthens the interfacial dipole and passivates halide vacancies, retaining 25.06% PCE on 1 cm2 devices with 3.11% relative efficiency loss (champion 25.75%) [@10.1002/adfm.76878], whereas co-assembling crown ethers at electron transport layer interfaces increases surface coverage from 0.57 to 0.79 and strengthens interfacial dipole moments from 2 to 7 Debye (champion 26.33% PCE) [@10.1002/adma.73886]. Polycyclic perylenocarbazole cores (6PCzBr) tailor electrostatic potentials for 26.20% PCE [@10.1002/anie.4031162], phosphine additives (TFMP) coordinate Pb2+ and stabilize formamidinium (FA+) in Me-4PACz and 4PABCz ternary contacts (champion 26.78% PCE) [@10.1002/adma.74416], and ammonium bromide interlayers (PFN-Br) tune band alignment for 23.15% PCE [@10.1002/smll.74806]. However, because most dipolar gains are reported on uncertified sub-0.1 cm2 cells without standardized bias protocols, whether these electrostatic barriers resist operational ion drift remains unverified.
