The quantitative frontier for operational durability is defined by time to 80% initial performance (T80) values of 9800 h [@10.1002/adma.74243] and 6785 h [@10.1002/aenm.71317], falling steeply to 720 h [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01637] and 200 h [@10.1002/smll.74531]. However, this frontier rests on narrow evidence: only 4 of 169 closely read studies report a T80 metric, and only 6 identify standardized International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability (ISOS) protocols, such as ISOS-O-1 [@10.1126/sciadv.aee4175], ISOS-L-1 [@10.1021/acsnano.6c04084] [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456], ISOS-L-3 [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01517], ISOS-L-1l [@10.1021/jacs.6c06932], or continuous maximum power point tracking (MPPT) [@10.26599/pom.2026.9140147]. Most stability claims instead rely on unstandardized, short-duration testing.

Depth-resolved profiling reveals that photo-induced degradation originates at both top and buried interfaces before penetrating the bulk [@10.1002/solr.70421]. To arrest interfacial chemical breakdown, molecularly tailored self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) stabilize buried contacts, yielding a certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 27.31% in single cells and 21.54% on a 766 cm2 module under ISOS-L-1 [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456], alongside asymmetric SAMs achieving 20.59% on 655.2 cm2 modules without certified durability [@10.1002/anie.1678852]. At the upper contact, multidentate fullerenes passivate defects and immobilize surface cations under ISOS-L-3, retaining 98.7% of initial PCE after 2100 h of continuous illumination and 95.8% after 1800 h at 85 °C, though on uncertified devices [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01517]. Interfacial defect passivation via aromatic carbonyls similarly claims stability but remains confined to an uncertified, sub-0.1 cm2 cell of 0.09 cm2 [@10.1002/anie.5216345]. In-situ two-dimensional perovskite reconstruction further blocks interfacial degradation [@10.1002/adma.74247] [@10.1002/solr.70447], while volatile dopants eliminate mobile lithium-ion residues in hole-transporting layers [@10.1002/smll.74289].

Under continuous bias and illumination, trapped-charge accumulation accelerates halide phase segregation, degrading wide-bandgap absorbers under standard MPPT, whereas maximum stability point tracking (MSPT) retains approximately 90% of initial power after 700 h [@10.1002/aenm.71358]. Ion-migration barriers from diammonium additives suppress segregation to achieve a certified 26.18% PCE on a 1.17 cm2 triple-junction aperture [@10.1038/s41467-026-75305-0], aligning with rubidium doping to homogenize vertical cation distributions under ISOS-L-1 [@10.1021/acsnano.6c04084]. These degradation pathways are intensified by dynamic thermo-mechanical stress fluctuations [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01837], where inelastic tensile stress pre-relaxation reduces moisture ingress by up to an order of magnitude [@10.26434/chemrxiv-2025-128tn/v2]. Without widespread adoption of standardized ISOS protocols, reported operational lifetimes remain poorly substantiated.
