The buried interface between charge-transporting contacts and polycrystalline perovskite absorbers dictates interfacial recombination kinetics and long-term device durability, yet it remains chemically and mechanically vulnerable [@10.1002/adfm.77486]. In inverted (p-i-n) architectures, carbazole-derived self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) anchored onto metal oxide transparent conducting layers frequently suffer from weak substrate chelation, inhomogeneous coverage, and parasitic pi-pi stacking that induces molecular aggregation. Addressing this requires transitioning from monodentate phosphonic acid anchoring toward multidentate coordination and steric engineering. Density functional theory calculations demonstrate that the noncoplanar multidentate contact molecule TTA suppresses pi-pi stacking-induced aggregation while reinforcing substrate and perovskite interactions, yielding a champion efficiency of 23.7% in wide-bandgap single-junction cells (approximately 1.67 eV) and a certified 33.1% (champion 33.3%) in perovskite/silicon tandems with no observed efficiency loss after 30 days of outdoor operation [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945]. Tetradentate chelation via Fe(III) porphyrins bearing four phosphonic-acid moieties similarly anchors to oxide substrates, producing a certified 26.82% (champion 27.06%) efficiency in small-area cells and 24.1% over a 21.54 cm2 aperture module [@10.1002/anie.8080769]. Tetraphosphorylated phthalocyanines also reinforce interfacial anchorage, achieving a certified 23.1% and stabilised 22.9% efficiency across a 20.3 cm2 module that endured 61 days of ISOS-O-1 outdoor testing without degradation [@10.1126/sciadv.aee4175].

The macroscopic consequence of weak molecular anchoring is low interfacial toughness and delamination under operating stress. Quantitative adhesion measurements reveal that SAM deposition on a NiOx underlayer raises interfacial fracture energy tenfold relative to bare indium tin oxide, and removing loosely bound SAM molecules increases interfacial fracture energy from 0.94 to 4.82 J m-2 [@10.1021/acsmaterialslett.6c00479]. Multidentate molecular mediators based on bisphosphonates rebuild the NiOx/SAM junction by passivating NiOx defects and coordinating undercoordinated Pb2+, yielding a certified 27.31% efficiency in small-area rigid cells and flexible modules that retain over 94% of their initial power after 3250 h of operation [@10.1002/adma.73807]. Zoledronic acid incorporates bisphosphonic acid head groups that anchor to the substrate, establish hydrogen bonding with SAMs, and passivate perovskite defect sites, contributing to a 30.62% champion tandem efficiency, although the wide-bandgap sub-cell was evaluated over only 200 h of maximum power point (MPP) tracking to demonstrate over 90% retention at an uncertified 20.55% efficiency [@10.1002/smll.74999]. Chemical desorption can also be arrested by incorporating Lindqvist-type polyoxometalates into SAMs on ITO, which raises the SAM anchoring ratio from 29.93% to 53.98%, lowers the desorption rate from 30.7% to 5.6%, and yields a 25.15% champion efficiency with 90.7% retention after 1200 h MPPT compared to 53.8% for untreated controls [@10.26599/pom.2026.9140147].

Beyond increasing coordination number, supramolecular assembly and steric modulation resolve the phase aggregation of carbazole contacts. Sulfonyl-functionalized calixarene establishes a supramolecular host-guest architecture at the buried interface, delivering a certified 27.12% small-area device and a certified 22.25% efficiency on a 655.2 cm2 aperture module while maintaining 97.4% performance after 2125 h of continuous one-sun operation [@10.1038/s41467-026-74018-8]. Incorporating the ionic liquid BMIMBF4 provides steric-electrostatic modulation that serves as a molecular lubricant to suppress SAM agglomeration, preserving 92.3% of an initial 26.82% champion efficiency after 800 h at 85 °C under AM 1.5G illumination [@10.1002/anie.7090517]. Dispersive stability can also be enforced through proton transfer complexation, which suppresses thermal degradation of carbazole SAMs at 75 °C for 1368 h and survives 100 thermal stress cycles between -60 °C and 85 °C in a 26.23% champion cell [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01489]. Thermally activated in situ polymerization networks that densely cover the SAM heterocontact similarly eliminate interfacial voids and pinholes to produce a 26.87% champion efficiency [@10.1002/anie.1018855]. Dynamic self-assembly integrating a binary SAM mixture and DMAEMA monomer into the perovskite precursor generates an in situ hole-transporting layer with a champion efficiency of 22.03%, Voc of 1.11 V, Jsc of 25.84 mA cm-2, and fill factor of 77% [@10.1021/acsami.6c09672]. Meanwhile, the multifunctional SAM T4 regulates the FTO/SAM/perovskite interface to attain a 26.67% champion efficiency with approximately 90% retention after 800 h of continuous MPPT [@10.1002/aenm.71282].

Defects at buried contacts arise not only from pinholes but from energetic misalignment and unpassivated ionic vacancies. Introducing fluorine substituents to replace methoxy groups in F-2PACz increases the interfacial dipole moment and coordinates halide vacancies, enabling 25.75% efficiency that translates to 25.06% on 1 cm2 devices with 3.11% relative loss and 92% retention after 1440 h under ISOS-L-3 protocols [@10.1002/adfm.76878]. Introducing a phosphine mediator, TFMP, into Me-4PACz and 4PABCz builds a ternary contact where the phosphine coordinates undercoordinated Pb2+ and stabilizes FA+ cations, reaching a 26.78% champion efficiency and 93.7% retention after 1200 h continuous MPPT [@10.1002/adma.74416]. Polycyclic aromatics such as 6PCzBr alter the electrostatic potential to attain 26.20% efficiency in inverted perovskite cells and 20.16% in organic photovoltaics [@10.1002/anie.4031162]. Interfacial interlayers of PFN-Br introduce bromide-counterion ammonium groups that suppress non-radiative recombination and adjust band alignment to achieve 23.15% efficiency [@10.1002/smll.74806]. At inorganic contacts, exchange of sulfonic acid groups with sodium ions via NaOAc in PEDOT:PSS elevates the work function from 4.27 to 4.38 eV, raises conductivity by 55%, and reduces unreacted Pb2+ defect density by 11%, attaining 19.38% efficiency with a fill factor above 81.7% and exceeding 21% in Sn-Pb mixed devices [@10.1021/acsami.6c08440]. Controlled oxygen partial pressure in Cu-doped NiOx films modulates the Ni3+ fraction to 0.76 at 10% PO2 and increases conductivity to 12.9 S/cm at 20% PO2, reducing trap density from 3.01e11 cm-3 to 1.71e11 cm-3 and yielding a 0.13 eV valence band offset, though overall efficiency remains modest at 13.7% [@10.1002/solr.70425]. Chemical degradation at organic contacts is highlighted by hard X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy of FAPbI3/spiro-OMeTAD interfaces, which reveals that spiro-OMeTAD deposition extracts Pb and I ions into the transport layer, shifts the N 1s core level toward downward band bending, and forms nonperovskite Pb species [@10.1021/acsami.6c08990]. Inserting MeO-2PACz at the perovskite/spiro-OMeTAD heterojunction mitigates this phase segregation and suppresses I2 expulsion [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01666], while supramolecular co-assemblies of CzPACl and CzOEG preserve Spiro-OMeTAD radical cations to retain over 90% efficiency after 1000 h continuous illumination and over 80% after 1000 h aging at 85 °C at 25.8% champion efficiency [@10.1002/anie.2884874].

At electron-selective buried contacts and upper interfaces, structural voids and residual lattice strain represent primary degradation pathways. Chelating agents PDA and PCA coordinate Pb2+ via phenanthroline nitrogen atoms to regulate DMSO solvent release, minimizing bottom void formation; however, the reported 25.15% champion efficiency is restricted to a 0.04 cm2 aperture area and aging data rely on unencapsulated air storage (>80% after 1,500 h) rather than continuous bias tracking [@10.1002/smll.74604]. Dipotassium salt DPMDS coordinates undercoordinated Sn4+ sites on SnO2 to create an interfacial dipole, while K+ ion migration into the absorber modulates crystallization to achieve 25.76% efficiency [@10.1002/adfm.77175]. Converting tensile strain into compressive strain through in situ growth of a 2D perovskite interlayer at the SnO2 junction achieves 25.67% efficiency with over 90% retention after 2500 h in air or 1000 h under illumination [@10.1002/adfm.76848]. Inorganic SnO2-GQDs provide conductive electron bridges, reaching 23.38% in Sn-Pb cells and 29.40% in all-perovskite tandems [@10.1002/aenm.71259]. Stabilizing the opposing perovskite/C60 electron transport layer with PTS via sulfonate anchoring and pyrene pi-pi stacking achieves a 26.53% champion efficiency, 96% retention after 1000 h MPPT, 91% retention after 300 thermal cycles from -40 to 85 °C, and scales to an 818 cm2 module exceeding 20% under ISOS-L-1l protocols [@10.1021/jacs.6c06932]. Hybrid interlayers of 4AA and DB18C6 increase surface coverage from 0.57 to 0.79 and interfacial dipole from 2 to 7 Debye, reaching 26.33% champion efficiency with 93.2% retention after 1000 h MPPT [@10.1002/adma.73886]. Semitransparent buried interfaces modified with NPAI demonstrate 21.4% efficiency with 61.3% average NIR transmittance over 800-1200 nm, enabling 27.7% four-terminal flexible tandems that endure 2000 bending cycles at 10 mm radius and 500 h MPPT [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01361]. In all-inorganic CsPbI3, BDA+5-FBA treatment elevates efficiency from 12.93% to 20.71% with a fill factor of up to 83.92% [@10.1002/smll.74356], while oxamide additives passivate iodide vacancies to reach 18.89% champion efficiency [@10.1002/smll.74390]. These empirical results contrast with SCAPS-1D simulations that report 30.81% efficiency in Pt/Cu2BaSnS4/FA-based/ZnO/ITO stacks [@10.1002/ente.70590] and 29.10% efficiency (Voc of 1.34 V, Jsc of 24.30 mA/cm2, and FF of 89.11%) in HTL-free FTO/ZnO/BaSnO3/(FAPbI3)0.85(MAPbBr3)0.15/Au devices [@10.1016/j.hybadv.2026.100721]; these computational projections assume defect-free bulk transport and disregard the chemical mixing, ion migration, and interfacial delamination observed in actual hardware.
