The putative defect tolerance of lead halide perovskites, attributed to dielectric screening and antibonding band extrema, contrasts sharply with operational degradation. Numerical simulations project optimistic efficiency ceilings under idealized defect passivity: one-dimensional SCAPS models predict 28.85% efficiency for lead-free CH3NH3SnI3 versus 21.10% for CH3NH3PbI3 with matched 20 nm transport layers [@10.1016/j.hybadv.2026.100722], tin dual absorbers reach a simulated 29.00% efficiency (Voc 1.01 V, Jsc 33.59 mA/cm2, FF 85.78%) [@10.1002/pip.70131], and triple-cation cells reach 29.08% (Voc 1.37 V, Jsc 23.86 mA cm-2, FF 88.79%) [@10.1016/j.hybadv.2026.100700]. Three-dimensional opto-electro-thermal models project 28.18% for MgZrS3, 26.72% for MgTiS3, and 28.16% for MgHfS3 (VOC 0.94-1.07 V, JSC 29.89-42.69 mA/cm2, FF 84.58-88.02%) [@10.48550/arxiv.2607.19789]. However, these continuum models neglect localized trap states, mobile ions, and interfacial strain, which dominate non-radiative recombination in actual devices and undermine bulk tolerance.

Interfacial defect remediation focuses on passivating undercoordinated lead cations and dangling bonds through directional coordination. Lewis acid-base complexation at the buried contact provides an initial barrier. In inverted cells, replacing standard self-assembled monolayers with BTP, a carbazole derivative with an extended conjugated scaffold and reduced sulfur electron density, passivates undercoordinated Pb2+ sites to achieve an uncertified 26.85% champion efficiency and retain 96% of initial efficiency after 1100 h at 65 °C [@10.1002/anie.1864800]. Machine-learning screening via SHAP analysis confirms that monolayer head-group dipole moments dominate charge extraction, identifying molecule S1 for an uncertified 26.21% efficiency on NiOx [@10.1002/adma.74060]. Multifunctional ligands broaden this defense. Post-treatment with small molecule TTA combines carboxyl coordination to dangling bonds with sulfur interactions with unreacted PbI2, retaining 92.1% of initial efficiency after 200 h at 70% relative humidity and 92% after 800 h under continuous AM 1.5G illumination, though the 26.7% champion efficiency remains uncertified [@10.1002/adma.73950]. At the electron-selective contact, post-deposition treatment with the multisite molecular salt SCP secures a certified efficiency of 26.84% (uncertified champion 27.25%, stabilised 26.84%), retaining 90% of initial performance after 1485 h of maximum-power-point tracking [@10.1002/aenm.71305]. Defect remediation also depends on spatial configuration: tetrakis(4-aminophenyl)methane incorporates a three-dimensional distribution of amino groups that coordinate both PbI2 and formamidinium iodide, retaining over 95% of initial efficiency after 1000 h of maximum-power-point tracking at 65 °C in nitrogen and translating an uncertified 26.26% cell efficiency (stabilised at 26.26%) to a 23.16% efficiency on a 14.0 cm2 module [@10.1038/s41467-026-75990-x]. Because surfaces host acidic and basic defects, ionic liquids with amphoteric anions such as GUHS and GUDHP simultaneously passivate Lewis acidic Pb2+ and Lewis basic halide defects, yielding an uncertified 26.13% champion efficiency, 20.06% efficiency with 85.74% fill factor in 1.84 eV wide-bandgap cells, and 93.6% retention after 1000 h under ISOS-L-1 protocols [@10.1002/adfm.77378]. In mixed tin-lead absorbers, combining PEASCN with amino acid hydrochlorides passivates defects and modulates the Fermi level to reach an uncertified 22.2% efficiency (Voc 0.86 V, FF 78.1%), yet stability remains poor, maintaining 80% efficiency over only 200 h because tin vacancy formation and Sn2+ oxidation proceed unabated [@10.1002/smll.74531].

Even with neutralized static traps, field-induced ion migration creates dynamic defects and interface degradation. Mobile iodide species accumulate at extraction layers under bias, accelerating failure. A dual iodide-trap strategy using TFIBA and FPIFA chemically sequesters mobile iodide to protect spiro-OMeTAD interfaces, yielding an uncertified 26.81% efficiency and a T93 lifetime over 1140 h at 85 °C [@10.1002/anie.2924886]. Compensating iodine vacancies and coordinating surface lead sites via FTAC homogenizes surface potential, retaining 91.16% of initial efficiency after 1,000 h at 85 °C in nitrogen and 88.67% after 1,000 h of maximum-power-point tracking at 65 °C in an uncertified 25.06% cell [@10.20517/energymater.2026.102]. Beyond halide migration, A-site cation movement undermines phase integrity. Dimensional capping layers address this instability: a unified ligand-dimensional architecture confines formamidinium cations within a 2D/3D heterojunction via geometric restriction and multidirectional hydrogen bonding, achieving a certified cell efficiency of 26.52% (uncertified champion 26.88%) and a certified 17.75% efficiency (uncertified champion 20.25%) across a 900 cm2 module, retaining over 91% of initial efficiency after 1000 hours at 85 °C and 85% relative humidity [@10.1126/sciadv.aed6327]. Halide imidazolium cations similarly form an interfacial 2D/3D heterojunction, retaining 92% of initial efficiency after 1200 h at 85 °C and 90% after 1000 h of illumination in an uncertified 26.33% device [@10.1002/adfm.77015]. In-situ spacer cation formation using HPA yields a 2D capping layer delivering an uncertified 26.32% efficiency on a 0.072 cm2 area and retaining 90% after 800 h at 85 °C in nitrogen [@10.1002/advs.76508]. However, low-dimensional capping layers frequently create out-of-plane transport barriers. Assembling 3D|2D stacks through colloidal suspensions avoids phase disruption and enables epitaxial alignment for 2D thicknesses up to 80 nm, raising efficiency from 24.2% to an uncertified 26.0% [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15006632/v1], while molten-salt DMTMM dissolution eliminates parasitic residual PbI2 to elevate efficiency from 23.43% to an uncertified 25.73% [@10.1002/adfm.76974]. Alternatively, choline-derivative intermediate-site anchoring ligands bypass 2D transport bottlenecks through nonlayered passivation, achieving an uncertified 26.54% efficiency, retaining over 90% efficiency after 2000 h at 65 °C and 90% relative humidity, and recording an experimental T80 of 9800 h with a projected thermal T80 of approximately 11,000 h [@10.1002/adma.74243].

Ion migration and defect generation are coupled to residual lattice strain, which lowers activation barriers for ion drift and accelerates degradation cascades. Photoresponsive C60-azo fullerenes undergo trans-to-cis isomerization under ultraviolet illumination to generate compressive stress that counterbalances residual tensile strain at the buried interface, retaining 92.7% of initial performance after 488 h of ultraviolet exposure and 94.7% after 1000 h of continuous maximum-power-point tracking in an uncertified 26.6% device [@10.1002/adma.74220]. Polymeric MPUA additives convert tensile strain into compressive strain during crystallization, driving an uncertified 24.91% efficiency [@10.1002/solr.70435]. Dual-site coordination by dithiopyr coordinates adjacent Pb2+ sites to relieve microstrain and promote (100) orientation, yielding a certified 26.14% cell efficiency (uncertified champion 26.9%) and 21.28% on a 30 × 30 cm2 module, while retaining over 95% of initial efficiency after 1100 h of maximum-power-point tracking [@10.1002/adma.74033]. Physical dissipation of mechanical stress through low-temperature liquid-assisted vibrational stress relief suppresses defect formation, achieving uncertified efficiencies of 26.47% on rigid cells, 24.64% on flexible substrates, 23.59% on a 16.8 cm2 module, and 20.55% on a 600 cm2 module, while retaining 92.4% of initial efficiency after 1200 hours of operation [@10.1038/s41467-026-75629-x]. Similarly, 2-amino-3-hydroxybutanoic acid cross-links the SnO2 interface to relieve interfacial strain and accelerate electron extraction to 3.5 ps, sustaining over 90% efficiency after 2000 h of ambient storage in an uncertified 26.32% cell [@10.1002/adfm.77373]. While solution-cast modifiers struggle with spatial uniformity during scaling, plasma surface engineering forms uniform, chemically bonded heterostructures that suppress defect accumulation at laser-scribed interconnects, yielding a certified module efficiency of 23.5% across 65.05 cm2 (uncertified champion cell 27.2%) and retaining 99.3% of initial efficiency after 1600 h at 65 °C under 1-sun illumination [@10.1126/science.aeg1730]. By comparison, simple phenethylammonium iodide on PCBM yields an uncertified 22.92% efficiency (Voc 1.18 V, FF 84.78%) with a modest -1.501 eV adsorption energy, but lacks long-term operational resilience, retaining 94.1% efficiency after only 150 h of continuous illumination [@10.1002/solr.70437].
