Independent certification establishes the verified baseline of photovoltaic performance by eliminating measurement artifacts that distort internal laboratory evaluations, such as spectral mismatch, scan-dependent capacitive currents, and aperture mischaracterization. Of 181 closely read papers, 22 reported an independently certified efficiency. This low rate of external verification reveals an evidentiary deficit across the literature, where the majority of reported device improvements and mechanistic assertions rest on uncalibrated, self-reported testing. Without independent calibration, apparent gains attributed to chemical passivation or interface engineering frequently reflect transient charge accumulation or spectral over-illumination rather than steady-state thermodynamic progress.

The certified frontier defines distinct operational boundaries across device architectures. In the single-junction p-i-n configuration, the certified ceiling reaches 33.1% [@10.1126/sciadv.aei2945]. This value stands well apart from the broader body of certified p-i-n devices, which exhibit consistent agreement at lower thresholds. Independent certification confirms 27.31% for planar p-i-n architecture [@10.1002/adma.73807]. An identical certified efficiency of 27.31% is reported in a separate p-i-n study [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456]. A certified efficiency of 27.3% establishes a closely aligned benchmark within this single-junction group [@10.1126/science.aed8175]. For two-terminal tandems, which encompass both perovskite/silicon and all-perovskite tandem configurations, the certified ceiling sits at 32.13% [@10.1002/anie.1676645]. Certified two-terminal tandem performance subsequently disperses across lower intervals. A certified efficiency of 29.57% reflects significant subcell recombination losses [@10.1002/adma.74078]. Another tandem device is certified at 28.84% [@10.1126/sciadv.aeb8790]. At the lower certified boundary, a two-terminal tandem achieves 27.49% [@10.1126/sciadv.aef6600]. The spread in certified tandem metrics reflects the physical sensitivity of series-connected subcells to current mismatch, optical parasitic absorption in recombination layers, and phase instability in wide-bandgap absorbers, where uncertified claims routinely obscure fill-factor losses under unstandardized spectral distributions.

A significant aspect of the verified frontier is the maintenance of high efficiencies across macroscopic dimensions. Scaled devices provide evidence that interfacial extraction and film continuity can survive large-area deposition without crippling series resistance. The largest reported area reaches 818 cm2 with a certified efficiency of 26.53% [@10.1021/jacs.6c06932]. Scaled p-i-n architecture achieves 27.31% on 766 cm2 [@10.1126/sciadv.aeg1456]. Equivalent performance is certified at 27.31% on an aperture of 684.75 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.73807]. Scaled module processing sustains 27.12% across 655.2 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-026-74018-8]. Area scaling also yields a certified efficiency of 27.8% across 625 cm2 [@10.1126/science.aed8175]. At intermediate scaling, a certified efficiency of 26.7% is achieved on 100 cm2 [@10.1126/science.aef1969]. These large-area certifications confirm that resistive dissipation through transparent conductive oxides and localized pinhole defects can be managed across hundreds of square centimeters.

The gap between the best certified values and the highest self-reported laboratory claims exposes a fundamental methodological divide. A self-reported champion value on a sub-0.1 cm2 cell and a certified value on a module are not the same kind of evidence and should not be compared directly. Devices operating on sub-0.1 cm2 apertures represent idealized local domains that avoid the dominant losses governing modules: lateral sheet resistance across extended transparent contacts, spatial non-uniformities in crystal growth, and geometric dead areas introduced by interconnection scribes. Small cells can also suffer from peripheral optical collection that inflates current density when aperture masks are improperly applied. Conversely, certified modules integrate these geometrical and resistive penalties, providing a realistic measure of macroscopic power conversion. When interpreting the 181 surveyed papers, the absence of independent certification in the vast majority of studies means that unverified champion claims cannot be taken as confirmed technological progress. Rigorous critical assessment requires separating certified, area-defined photovoltaic benchmarks from unverified microscale demonstrations.
