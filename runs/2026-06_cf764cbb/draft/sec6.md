Accelerated operational testing frequently fails to distinguish extrinsic contact corrosion, edge ingress, and phase segregation from intrinsic absorber degradation [@10.1016/joule.2026.102538] [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c00904] [@10.1002/solr.70390]. In triple-junction perovskite/perovskite/silicon cells, unencapsulated outdoor operation degrades efficiency to 80% after 4 months and to 50% after 6 months [@10.1002/solr.70390]. This diagnostic ambiguity is compounded by the scarcity of standardized lifetime evaluations: only 6 of 167 closely read papers reported a time to 80% of initial efficiency (T80) and only 3 named an International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability (ISOS) protocol, such as ISOS-D-1 [@10.1021/acsaem.6c00778] or ISOS-D [@10.1016/j.cej.2026.177834].

At electron-selective contacts, phenyl-C61-butyric acid methyl ester (PCBM) degrades via photo-induced [2+2] cycloaddition, causing dimerization and morphological instability that steric hindrance from nonplanar additives suppresses, reaching an uncertified champion power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 26.6% [@10.1002/solr.70405]. At heterointerfaces, bulky anionic passivators induce thermal lattice expansion, whereas reactive reconstruction and embedded passivation mitigate this fatigue, enabling unencapsulated devices to achieve a certified 27.28% efficiency with a 3566 h T80 and retaining 87% of initial efficiency after 4092 h of maximum power point tracking (MPPT) under ISOS-L-1 [@10.1126/sciadv.aef6596]. Degradation of self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) during perovskite deposition is mitigated by transient two-dimensional capping layers [@10.1002/adma.73561] or hydroxylated MXenes on nickel oxide [@10.1002/adma.73656], though both demonstrations remain restricted to sub-0.1 cm2 apertures of 0.05 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.73561] and 0.0524 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.73656].

In the bulk absorber, deep-level interstitial defects dominate non-radiative degradation; electropositive ion passivation reduces rear-interface energy loss from 1.46% to 0.62% to yield a certified 25.56% efficiency [@10.1002/adma.73679]. Relieving lattice microstrain stabilizes mixed-cation absorbers, enabling unencapsulated 12 cm2 devices to retain over 94.36% of initial efficiency after 1500 h under ISOS-L-1I and 80.5% after 1000 h at 85°C under ISOS-D-2I [@10.1002/adfm.76756]. Conversely, ionic sulfur anchoring of lithium dopants preserves approximately 90% of initial efficiency after 800 h of MPPT, but this remains demonstrated only on an uncertified 0.04 cm2 champion cell reaching 26.09% [@10.1002/adfm.76257].
