Investigations into monolithic two-terminal (2T) perovskite/silicon tandems diverge on whether a laterally conductive transparent conducting oxide (TCO) interlayer is required for subcell recombination. Conformal coverage on textured silicon heterojunction (SHJ) bottom cells reportedly required an 8 nm indium tin oxide (ITO) layer modified with nickel oxide (NiOx) and a polymeric self-assembled monolayer (Poly-SAM), gaining 0.85 mA cm-2 in short-circuit current density (JSC) to reach 20.82 mA cm-2 in a 31.8% champion cell [@10.1002/adfm.75311]. Conversely, omitting indium-based oxides entirely, a reactive plasma deposition (RPD) tin oxide (SnOx) recombination layer directly anchored self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) to suppress halide migration and nonradiative recombination, achieving certified 33.6% power conversion efficiency (PCE) (33.2% champion on 1 cm2) and certified 31.0% on a 207.9 cm2 minimodule [@10.1126/science.aef5355]. The physical controversy centers on whether removing ITO lateral conductivity creates localized resistive bottlenecks across textured pyramids. Spatially resolved voltage-dependent photoluminescence (PL) imaging via the local transport and recombination study (LTRS) method settles this debate: by mapping series resistance (Rs) to isolate an absolute 1.9% efficiency loss in a 29% monolithic tandem while decoupling current mismatch, low shunt resistance, and luminescence coupling in finite element simulations, operando LTRS determines whether TCO-free junctions exacerbate resistive losses under spectral variation [@10.1002/smll.202513958].

Parasitic optical absorption within selective contacts further constrains current matching. Thinning electron-selective fullerene (C60) contacts to 1 nm yields thickness-independent extraction, boosting tandem JSC by 0.24 mA cm-2 in a 31.7% champion cell [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c04082]. Yet atomic layer deposition (ALD) of tin oxide damages underlying perovskite during C60 capping; substituting ALD with thermally evaporated antimony oxide eliminates damage while permitting thinner fullerenes, improving top-cell JSC by ~1 mA cm-2 (certified 27.7%, champion 28.16% on 64.64 cm2) [@10.1038/s41467-026-70848-8]. In normal (n-i-p) tandems, an ultrathin 10 nm dopant-free 2,2',7,7'-tetrakis[N,N-di(4-methoxyphenyl)amino]-9,9'-spirobifluorene (spiro-OMeTAD) layer reduces parasitic absorption by 92.2% and reflection losses by 18.4%, delivering an uncertified 29.73% PCE on 0.135 cm2 and 28.77% (certified 28.25%) on 1.012 cm2 [@10.1007/s40820-025-01962-3]. Decoupling current matching, four-terminal (4T) architectures using semitransparent 1.67 eV wide-bandgap (WBG) subcells with vanadium oxide-nickel oxide (VOx-NiOx) contacts achieved 21.68% on 0.148 cm2, retaining 98% efficiency after 1472 h maximum power point tracking (MPPT) at 25 °C (31.25% tandem on 1 cm2) [@10.1002/aenm.71036], while priming CsPbX3 intermediate phases yielded 20.91% on 0.1 cm2 and 19.67% on 1.0 cm2 (31.04% tandem on 1.0 cm2) [@10.1038/s41467-026-72094-4].

Matching bottom cells requires WBG absorbers (1.67-1.85 eV), where drift-diffusion modeling indicates that bromide substitution and cesium alloying in formamidinium lead triiodide (FAPbI3) increase carrier effective mass from 0.173 me to 0.243-0.258 me, restricting transport despite projected 32.10% PCE and 2.05 V open-circuit voltage (VOC) [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15001694/v1]. Approaches over the six-month period diverge on additive chemistry. Several groups introduced fluorinated agents: difluoro(oxalato)borate and tetrafluoroborate synchronized crystallization without intermediate halide redistribution, certifying a 30.3% tandem [@10.1038/s41565-026-02165-6]; volatile octafluoronaphthalene (OFNA) relieved tensile stress and passivated defects (22.23% single junction, 31.43% tandem) [@10.1002/adfm.202526009]; and 4-(trifluoromethyl)benzenesulfonyl chloride (BSCF3) coordinated lead and formamidinium (19.81% single junction, 1.32 V VOC, 27.26% tandem) [@10.1002/solr.70387]. In direct conflict, tracking ion diffusion revealed that mobile fluoride from lithium fluoride (LiF) interlayers penetrates the perovskite bulk, accelerating halide segregation and degrading photostability; an ion-blocking lithium-theophylline (Li-T) interlayer was required to halt fluoride migration, securing 1.284 V VOC and 19.55% efficiency in 1.77 eV cells (28.41% tandem) [@10.1002/aenm.202506634]. Non-fluorinated strategies instead employed forchlorfenuron (CPPU) to direct (100) facets (certified 23.46% single junction, 1.295 V VOC; certified 31.93%, champion 32.17% tandem on 1.0 cm2) [@10.1016/j.esci.2026.100563], 1,2-diiodotetrafluorobenzene (1,2-DITFB) to retard bromide crystallization (20.25% single junction, 1.34 V VOC, 28.58% tandem) [@10.1002/adfm.74659], phenylhydrazine derivatives to inhibit iodide oxidation (20.75% single junction, 28.63% tandem) [@10.1002/smll.202510652], and 2-phenylethylammonium chloride (PEACl) modification with solvent etching (CDSE) to remove residual lead iodide (PbI2) (21.77% single junction, 31.85% tandem) [@10.1002/aenm.202506619]. Rubidium cyanate (RbOCN) lattice incorporation stabilized 1.73 eV absorbers (22.45% single junction, 25.37% on 1 cm2, 26.75% champion on an uncertified 0.09 cm2 area, 2.22 V VOC) [@10.1002/adma.72986], while targeted passivator coordination achieved a certified 2.00 V tandem VOC (certified 31.52%, champion 32.74%) [@10.1002/aenm.71077]. In contrast, additive-free stoichiometry control via a PbI2-to-total PbX2 ratio of ~55-75% slowed crystallization to deliver single-junction PCEs of ~24.19% (1.67 eV) to 16.83% (1.93 eV) and a 29.1% tandem [@10.1021/acsnano.6c04740]. Evaporated scaffolds expose a mechanistic disconnect: two-step thermal evaporation yielded stabilized tandem PCEs above 29% [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c00156], yet five sequentially evaporated scaffold stacks produced identical ~19% single-junction PCEs (28.4% champion tandem) despite profound variations in precursor conversion and morphology [@10.1021/acsami.6c07582].

At buried hole-selective junctions, composite SAMs of 4,4',4'',4'''-methanetetrayltetrabenzoic acid (4MA) with [4-(3,6-dimethyl-9H-carbazol-9-yl)butyl]phosphonic acid (Me-4PACz) on NiOx use tetrahedral molecular pinning to passivate defects, achieving 23.52% in 1.68 eV cells and a certified 32.1% (32.26% champion on 1 cm2) tandem [@10.1002/anie.5162616]. Aligning packing via intermolecular pi-pi interactions in [2-(3,6-dimethyl-9H-carbazol-9-yl)ethyl]diphenylphosphine oxide (Me-Ph2mPACz) reduced non-radiative losses from 168 mV to 124 mV (single-junction PCE 21.82% to 23.14%), delivering a certified 32.45% (33.4% champion) tandem retaining 83% efficiency after 1000 h MPPT at 85 °C in air [@10.1038/s41467-026-72160-x]. Three-dimensional C-H...pi transport networks from asymmetric MeO-BADCz SAMs certified at 33.04% (24.05% champion reported) [@10.1038/s41467-026-73656-2], and the triazole-based quaternary ammonium molecule TMTEA co-regulated SAM packing and orientation (23.93% single junction, 32.15% champion) [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c00166]. Under operational current mismatch, subcells are driven into reverse bias. In all-perovskite tandems combining WBG and narrow-bandgap (NBG) absorbers, sustained reverse bias induces a destructive redox cascade: hole injection oxidizes iodide to iodine species that oxidize Sn2+ to Sn4+, driving lattice collapse in a 29.03% tandem [@10.1002/adma.202522942].
