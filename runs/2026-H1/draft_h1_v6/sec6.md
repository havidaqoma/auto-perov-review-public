Recent module research exposes a direct contradiction regarding buried contact chemistry: whether large-area crystallization requires intentional metal excess to direct unidirectional growth, or strict stoichiometric neutrality to eliminate trap-assisted recombination. One approach demonstrates that an engineered lead-rich (Pb-rich) buried interface enforces upward unidirectional crystallization from the substrate, eliminating buried voids and delivering an uncertified 21.56% champion power conversion efficiency (PCE) across a 10.08 cm2 carbon-electrode perovskite solar module (PSM) [@10.1002/adma.73502]. Conversely, area-selective atomic layer deposition (ALD) studies demonstrate that undercoordinated interfacial lead atoms act as non-radiative recombination centers rather than benign growth directors. Passivating that lead with aluminium oxide (AlOx) achieved an uncertified 26.41% champion PCE across 64.68 cm2 [@10.1002/anie.202516537]. This divergence extends across alternative contact strategies. Mesoscopic chemical reconfiguration of the tin oxide (SnO2) electron transport layer achieved a certified 26.64% PCE (26.7% champion) on 21.5 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.73286], while redox-mediated buried interface stabilization yielded an uncertified 26.48% champion PCE on 11.0 cm2 [@10.1002/anie.4012708]. Resolving whether residual interfacial lead directs crystallization or accelerates phase segregation requires operando, cross-sectional photoluminescence (PL) lifetime imaging coupled with nanoscale secondary ion mass spectrometry (nanoSIMS) across module stripe widths under continuous bias.

Scalable meniscus coating reveals an analogous conflict in fluid mechanics: whether large-area uniformity requires prolonged drying to suppress Marangoni flows or rapid extraction to bypass intermediate solvate phases. Suppressing coffee-ring flows through fluid-locking interfacial layers yielded an uncertified 26.61% champion PCE [@10.1002/adma.202518582]. Pairing fluid dynamics with ambient-printed self-assembled monolayer (SAM) interlayers achieved a certified 25.85% PCE (26.31% champion) across 13.04 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.73373]. Conversely, slot-die processing of formamidinium lead iodide (FAPbI3) across 59.5 cm2 required forced solvent extraction to suppress yellow-phase formation, attaining an uncertified 20.8% champion PCE [@10.1021/acsami.5c22032]. Solvent formulation deepens this contrast. Localized solidification control with green colloidal inks produced an uncertified 22.3% champion PCE across 31.5 cm2 [@10.1002/aenm.202504928]. Chemical soaking baths designed to rapidly quench wide-bandgap films instead produced an uncertified 22.03% champion PCE over 10.13 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-026-69264-9], matching frameworks for roll-to-roll slot-die coating [@10.1002/advs.202522103]. These contradictory processing windows show that scalable printing lacks a unified model relating ink rheology, evaporation rate, and nucleation kinetics.

Molecular design of hole-selective SAMs targets series resistance across extended substrates, yet consensus is lacking on whether molecular rigidity or mechanical compliance governs scalability. Imposing rotational constraints on phosphonic acid anchors aligns interfacial dipoles and minimizes potential fluctuations, yielding a certified 26.31% PCE (26.87% champion) across a 47.84 cm2 aperture [@10.1002/aenm.71068]. Asymmetric polymeric SAMs homogenize buried energetics to deliver an uncertified 26.13% champion PCE on 57.6 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-026-73276-w]. Ionic SAMs establish neutral interfaces that produce an uncertified 26.88% champion PCE across 29.7 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-026-74288-2]. However, rigid dipolar ordering often sacrifices interfacial mechanical toughness. Incorporating benzofuran-substituted molecules improves adhesion but caps the uncertified champion PCE at 21.2% on 10.0 cm2 [@10.1002/smll.74000], while bilateral anchoring strategies attempt to balance charge transfer and adhesion [@10.1002/aenm.71001]. The persistent efficiency roll-off with area indicates that wetting heterogeneity and pinholes through the SAM layer dominate scaling losses over intrinsic dipole alignment.

Thermomechanical stress represents an escalating barrier to module longevity, as lattice expansion mismatches accelerate defect formation. Homogenizing the out-of-plane strain distribution across the absorber yielded a certified 25.55% PCE (26.59% champion) across a 100 cm2 flexible module under International Summit on Organic Photovoltaics Stability (ISOS) ISOS-L-1 illumination [@10.1126/sciadv.aec3238]. Regulating crystallization-defect-strain interactions in formamidinium-cesium (FACs) alloys produced a certified 26.12% PCE (26.76% champion) across 12 cm2 [@10.1002/adfm.76756]. Cross-linking scaffolds reinforce grain boundaries against strain-induced degradation: a sodium-integrated cross-linkable scaffold enabled a certified 26.75% PCE (26.9% champion) on 10.24 cm2 [@10.1002/anie.202525815], while cross-linked n+-type interlayers achieved a certified 26.27% PCE (26.34% champion) over 14 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-026-72793-y]. Fluorinated self-polymerizable additives passivate halide vacancies for an uncertified 26.01% champion PCE on 14 cm2 [@10.1021/acsenergylett.5c03901]. Conjugated imidazolium additives inhibit ion migration to achieve an uncertified 25.63% champion PCE on 20.25 cm2 [@10.1002/smll.202514478]. Vapor-phase grain-boundary anchoring provides bending endurance on flexible substrates, though uncertified champion PCE remains at 15.7% across 20.25 cm2 [@10.1002/anie.7292679].

Across the 43 papers in this module family, the area penalty remains severe. Single-junction small cells reach 27.6% certified PCE on areas up to 4 cm2, whereas modules exhibit a median certified PCE of 26.27%, with only 23.3% of studies reporting independent certification and 69.8% stating an active area. The highest certified module efficiency was 26.8% (27.12% uncertified champion), reached with a self-healing dynamic-bond scaffold across 655.2 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.73855]. Dual-targeted anchoring delivered an uncertified 26.62% champion PCE across 651 cm2 under verified ISOS-L-1 testing [@10.1002/aenm.71111]. In-situ dual-stage reactions sustained operation at an uncertified 23.3% champion PCE over 694.5 cm2 [@10.1002/solr.70353]. Modular auxetic metamaterials integrating silver nanowire (AgNW) and graphene networks reached 900 cm2, the largest area in the family [@10.1002/adfm.202532205]. Scribe-line dead zones and thermal edge degradation constitute intrinsic area costs. Controlling crystallization kinetics at laser scribe lines achieved a certified 23.55% PCE (24.7% champion) on 100 cm2 under ISOS-D-1 dark storage [@10.1038/s41467-026-69685-6]. Scribing and geometry adjustments also support vapour-treated printed carbon modules [@10.1016/j.decarb.2026.100161] and optical front layers for bifacial modules delivering an 18.48% certified PCE [@10.1016/j.esci.2026.100601].

Low-dimensional heterojunctions and composition pinning provide chemical stabilization, yet standardized stability reporting remains rare: only 4.7% of module papers reported a time to 80% of initial performance (T80), and only 7.0% (3 papers) followed defined ISOS protocols. Intercalated 2D/3D perovskite heterojunctions achieved an uncertified 26.22% champion PCE across 6.8 cm2 with a T80 of 1050 h, the longest lifetime in this family [@10.1007/s40820-025-02058-8]. Non-Dion-Jacobson 2D capping layers achieved an uncertified 26.52% champion PCE over 50 cm2 [@10.1002/anie.2974414]. Thermotropic liquid interface regulation yielded a certified 25.62% PCE on 22.8 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-025-68231-0], and composition pinning in lead-tin perovskites delivered an uncertified 19.37% champion PCE across 64 cm2 [@10.1002/inf2.70153]. Semi-transparent cesium lead bromide (CsPbBr3) modules reached an uncertified 7.78% champion PCE across 64 cm2 [@10.1016/j.cej.2026.176242]. Tandem module implementations indicate that scaling penalties persist across multi-junction geometries relative to 16 cm2 hybrid tandems (33.6% certified) and 64.64 cm2 all-perovskite tandems (30.3% certified). Four-terminal perovskite/silicon tandem modules with MXene junctions achieved an uncertified 19.45% champion PCE across 60 cm2 [@10.1038/s41467-026-70002-4], and perovskite-organic tandems reached an uncertified 19.89% champion PCE across 16.41 cm2 [@10.1002/adfm.202532203]. Finally, indoor photovoltaics (IPVs) function under narrow low-flux spectra and must not be benchmarked against one-sun metrics. Fully evaporated mini-modules reached an uncertified 41.6% champion PCE under indoor lighting, specifically 41.60% at 900 lux and 41.22% at 300 lux across 3.9 cm2 [@10.1002/smtd.70654], while semi-transparent indoor modules delivered an uncertified 13.78% champion PCE with a 268 h T80 [@10.1002/aenm.202505931].
