Solution deposition of hole-selective self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) on textured silicon bottom cells suffers from inhomogeneous conformality, where uneven coverage of [2-(3,6-dimethoxy-9H-carbazol-9-yl)ethyl]phosphonic acid (MeO-2PACz) precipitates residual lead iodide (PbI2) and causes interfacial shunting [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102645] [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01966]. Cooperative SAM strategies incorporating co-adsorbents with a methoxy terminal group and flexible alkyl chain improve substrate wettability and molecular packing on pyramids, lifting the uncertified power conversion efficiency (PCE) of two-terminal (2T) perovskite/silicon tandems with 1.68 eV wide-bandgap (WBG) absorbers to 32.16% [@10.1002/aenm.71512]. To prevent degradation during atomic layer deposition (ALD), sacrificial coordination suppresses SAM aggregation via hydrogen bonding and shields the anchored monolayer, enhancing adhesion (ASTM D3359) to achieve an operational T90 of 1265 hours and a certified PCE of 26.56% (champion 27.03%) on a miniature 0.062 cm2 aperture [@10.1002/adma.74461]. Inorganic hole-transport layer (HTL) stacks offer an alternative to vulnerable organophosphonates: pairing nickel oxide (NiOx) with dye N719 achieves 96.4% PCE retention after one year of outdoor maximum power point tracking (MPPT) [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01363]. In polymeric HTLs, dilauroyl peroxide doping of poly[bis(4-phenyl)(2,4,6-trimethylphenyl)amine] (PTAA) raises the water contact angle from 60.18° to 80.39°, boosting champion efficiency to 24.82% in n-i-p and 26.04% in p-i-n perovskite solar cells (PSCs) [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c02136].

Concurrently, disordered nucleation, strain, and ion drift across textured pyramids compromise WBG absorber stability [@10.1002/adfm.202532038] [@10.5281/zenodo.21790600]. Pre-depositing a cesium chloride (CsCl) seed layer across uneven SAMs homogenizes organic precursor incorporation and eliminates interfacial PbI2, securing a certified PCE of 29.7% (30.3% champion) [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102645]. Solvent-free sequential vapor evaporation avoids liquid dewetting entirely; in situ X-ray diffraction (XRD) confirms accelerated lead halide conversion on textured silicon over planar substrates, yielding a stabilised 27.0% PCE [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102646]. Post-deposition healing with methylammonium thiocyanate (MASCN) induces secondary crystallization into columnar grains spanning the full film thickness, enabling an encapsulated 1.164 cm2 device to achieve a certified 30.77% PCE and retain initial performance over 3400 hours of continuous one-sun MPPT in ambient conditions [@10.1038/s41467-026-76713-y]. To counteract mechanical distortion, two-dimensional (2D) perovskite seeding directs regular compressive strain into the three-dimensional (3D) WBG lattice, delivering 20.83% PCE with an 84.72% fill factor (FF) in single junctions and 29.47% in tandems [@10.1021/acsnano.6c07355]. In the bulk absorber, ion modulation elevates the ion migration activation barrier, lifting uncertified champion tandem PCE to 33.02% from 31.12% for control devices [@10.1002/adfm.77637].

At the tandem interconnect, optical and resistive losses restrict device operation. In perovskite-organic tandems, a zinc sulfide (ZnS) interlayer converts isolated gold clusters into laterally planarized domains, establishing low-loss recombination pathways at only ~77.4% metallic coverage while suppressing localized surface plasmon resonance to yield a 27.1% champion PCE and a T80 of 1400 hours [@10.1002/anie.6548693]. Translating 2T tandems beyond 33%-34% laboratory benchmarks toward commercial modules requires overcoming texturing shunts, near-infrared (NIR) parasitic absorption, balance-of-system (BOS) costs, and the defect cascades that penalize large-area scaling [@10.1002/adfm.202532038] [@10.5281/zenodo.21790600].
