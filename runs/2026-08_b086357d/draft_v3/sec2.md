Independent verification remains rare across current publications: only 18 of 144 closely read papers reported an independently certified power conversion efficiency (PCE). For two-terminal (2T) tandems, independent certification established a 33.66% ceiling for perovskite/silicon tandems alongside certified efficiencies of 32.56% and 30.77% [@10.1038/s41467-026-76266-0] [@10.1002/anie.5241611] [@10.1038/s41467-026-76713-y], whereas all-perovskite tandem devices reached certified boundaries of 29.7% and 27.84% [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102645] [@10.1038/s41467-026-76786-9]. In single-junction positive-intrinsic-negative (p-i-n) devices, non-radiative recombination suppression defines a certified ceiling of 27.4%, closely corroborated by certified metrics of 27.2% and 26.77% [@10.1126/science.aeg8415] [@10.1038/s41467-026-76497-1] [@10.1002/anie.3095986].

A self-reported champion on a sub-0.1 cm2 cell and a certified value on a module are different classes of evidence. Self-reported metrics run substantially ahead of certified baselines because uncertified testing on sub-0.1 cm2 apertures artificially minimizes series resistance, avoids lateral coating non-uniformities, and omits interconnection dead space. Uncertified claims at larger areas illustrate this tension: self-reported efficiencies reached 27.7% on 25 cm2 and 27.01% on 58.51 cm2 [@10.1038/s43246-026-01321-8] [@10.1002/adma.74469], rivaling certified small-area cells. However, uncertified performance across similar apertures diverges steeply due to sheet-resistance and defect-density penalties, falling to 23.3% on 57.3 cm2, 23.14% on 29.7 cm2, and 17.35% on 61.2 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.74629] [@10.1002/advs.76950] [@10.1002/smll.75068]. Without independent verification across macroscopic apertures, uncertified records mask interfacial heterogeneity and distort true progress.
