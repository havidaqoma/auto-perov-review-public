Buried contacts in inverted (p-i-n) and regular (n-i-p) perovskite solar cells (PSCs) govern non-radiative recombination and charge extraction, yet unpassivated defects, band misalignment, and interfacial voiding compromise performance [@10.1007/s40820-026-02327-0] [@10.1002/solr.70473] [@10.3390/nano16160989]. At metal-oxide electron-transport layers (ETLs), reactive surface species trigger mechanical voiding; gas-solid ethanol vapor reconstruction converts tin oxide (SnO2) surface hydroxyls into anchored acetate and passivates oxygen vacancies for an uncertified 26.19% champion power conversion efficiency (PCE) [@10.1002/smll.75122]. Subliming volatile pyruvic acid at 145 °C similarly cleans SnO2 to lower oxygen defects, delivering a 26.17% champion PCE on a 1 cm2 aperture with an 80% initial performance retention (T80) of 900 hours [@10.1002/aenm.71514]. Bidentate bridges such as 3,4-diaminobenzoic acid (DABA) bridge this boundary by anchoring carboxyl groups to Sn4+ while passivating undercoordinated lead (Pb2+) defects with amino groups [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01436].

At hole-transport layers (HTLs), interfacial acidity and defect states provoke analogous degradation. In methylammonium-free lead-tin devices, 4-(trifluoromethyl)benzamidine hydrochloride (TFBA) suppresses acid-induced degradation by inhibiting sulfonate (-SO3H) deprotonation on poly(styrenesulfonate) (PSS), securing an uncertified 22.48% champion PCE [@10.1002/smll.75089]. On nickel oxide (NiOx), ammonium thiocyanate (NH4SCN) consumes surface hydroxyls and relieves lattice strain for a 21.41% champion PCE [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01708]. Decoupling self-assembled monolayer (SAM) chemisorption from bulk transport via a structural-compensation stack of sputtered NiOx and solution-processed hydroxyl-rich overlayers yields a 24.86% champion PCE across a 12.4 cm2 aperture [@10.1002/smll.75056], while enamine-based SAM anchor engineering achieves 23.14% PCE and 83.11% fill factor (FF) on a 29.7 cm2 mini-module [@10.1002/advs.76950].

However, SAM hole contacts frequently suffer from aggregation and dipole misalignment. Non-anchoring 1,3,5-tris(trifluoromethyl)benzene (TTMB) suppresses SAM aggregation while preserving substrate binding sites, reducing recombination and shunting to yield certified and stabilised 33.66% PCEs in perovskite/silicon tandems [@10.1038/s41467-026-76266-0]. In wide-bandgap cells, building an interfacial hydrogen-bond network with glycinamide hydrochloride suppresses molecular clustering in [4-(3,6-dimethoxy-9H-carbazol-9-yl)butyl]phosphonic acid (MeO-4PACz) for a 22.38% champion PCE [@10.1016/j.decarb.2026.100190]. Asymmetric sulfur- and chlorine-functionalized molecules tune buried band offsets to achieve champion PCEs of 26.49% (25.70% steady-state) [@10.1002/adfm.77712] and 26.38% [@10.1002/aenm.71482], while co-assembling dipoles on NiOx yields a 20.36% four-terminal tandem champion PCE [@10.1038/s41467-026-76847-z]. Yet dipolar engineering often lacks rigorous validation: bismuth oxychloride (BiOCl) flips interfacial dipoles alongside chlorine-iodine exchange to yield a certified 27.84% tandem PCE (champion 28.14%, stabilised 27.86%), but single-junction inverted devices reaching 27.25% (1.55 eV) and 20.24% (1.85 eV) PCE were restricted to an unscaled 0.09 cm2 area [@10.1038/s41467-026-76786-9], while dipole tuning with 2-fluorobenzene-1,3-dicarboxylic acid (m-FBC) reached a 25.03% champion PCE whose 94% retention after 500 hours derived from unlabelled storage [@10.1021/acsami.6c11467].

Buried contacts also suffer thermomechanical failure under operational stress. Anchoring [60]fullerene-phosphonic acid (C60-PA) on SnO2 elevates maximum power point (MPP) tracked efficiency from an average of 18.6% to 20.0%, yet degrades under illuminated heating because fullerenes weaken interfacial adhesion [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01485]. Strengthening adhesion requires molecular templating: pairing precursor additives with phosphonic acid SAMs enhances interfacial adhesion and suppresses groove depths for a 26.58% champion PCE [@10.1002/anie.6932848], while self-assembled multilayers with [2-(10-(3,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-7H-benzo[c]carbazol-7-yl)ethyl]phosphonic acid (DMPA) secure certified 27.2% and champion 27.59% PCEs, retaining 94.5% efficiency after 1600 hours at 65 °C under 1 Sun illumination [@10.1038/s41467-026-76497-1]. Coordinate bonding offers comparable reinforcement: thienyl-based additives template (111) crystallization and segregate at grain boundaries to form passivating lead-sulfur (Pb-S) bonds, yielding a certified 32.56% (champion 33.0%) tandem PCE [@10.1002/anie.5241611]. Pb-S coordination via dibutyl sulfide induces a surface p-to-n transition for an uncertified 26.28% champion PCE [@10.1021/acsami.6c07884], while multi-arm donor-acceptor dipole bridges coordinate Pb2+ via C=N and methoxy groups to relieve residual strain, yielding a 26.79% champion PCE [@10.1002/anie.3716107].
