The scaling of perovskite solar cells (PSCs) from laboratory coupons to large-area modules incurs an efficiency penalty dominated by spatial mass-transfer disparities, stochastic nucleation, and resistive or environmental losses. Non-uniform drying across decimeter areas induces pinholes and phase segregation; replacing vertical diffusion-limited solvent evaporation with convective or engineered antisolvent extraction suppresses these defects [@10.1002/smll.75068] [@10.1038/s43246-026-01321-8] [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c02178]. Closed-loop slit-flushing antisolvent deposition imposes horizontal forced convection over 100 × 100 mm substrates to achieve a certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 17.35% on a 61.2 cm2 module [@10.1002/smll.75068]. Static antisolvent bathing with isopropanol admixtures homogenizes crystallization into larger crystalline nanodomains verified by grazing-incidence wide-angle X-ray scattering (GIWAXS), enabling an uncertified 27.7% champion PCE on a 25 cm2 two-terminal (2T) tandem device [@10.1038/s43246-026-01321-8], while polar additives in slot-die precursors yield 30 nm films with 1.86 nm roughness over 100 cm2 [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c02178]. In parallel, chemical coordination and heterogeneous seeding regulate nucleation barriers to prevent premature precipitation [@10.1002/adma.74469] [@10.1002/anie.7848549] [@10.1021/acsami.6c11722]. Polyfluoroarene additives elevate effective nucleation barriers to convert rapid aggregation into uniform film growth, achieving an uncertified module champion PCE of 27.01% on 58.51 cm2 with small-area non-radiative voltage losses of 55 mV [@10.1002/adma.74469]. Purine tautomers alter lead-nitrogen coordination and hydrogen bonding, confirmed by density functional theory (DFT), yielding a certified 26.51% PCE (champion 27.05%) at 12.95 cm2 [@10.1002/anie.7848549], whereas cesium lead triiodide (CsPbI3) nanocube seeding enforces controlled crystallization and depth-dependent Cs+ incorporation in slot-die coated formamidinium lead triiodide (FAPbI3) [@10.1021/acsami.6c11722]. Beyond nucleation, solid-state transformation barriers and resistive contacts limit module performance [@10.1002/adma.74828] [@10.1021/acsnano.5c21962]. Laser-structured channels in lead iodide (PbI2) provide deterministic diffusion pathways that lower phase transformation activation barriers, reaching an uncertified 22.83% PCE across 22.95 cm2 modules [@10.1002/adma.74828]. For charge extraction, cerium iodide (CeI3) interfacial modifiers establish inorganic hole contacts, yielding 25.19% PCE in 0.113 cm2 cells and 23.37% in 21 cm2 modules with a 98.3% geometric fill factor (GFF) [@10.1021/acsnano.5c21962]. Finally, scalable slot-die processing remains constrained by environmental sensitivity and mechanical degradation [@10.1021/acsaem.6c02083] [@10.1021/acsami.6c07537]. Ambient humidity induces marked PCE degradation when exceeding 30% relative humidity (RH) [@10.1021/acsaem.6c02083], yet incorporating Ti3C2Tx MXene nanospheres extends continuous illumination stability from 400 h to over 1700 h in all-slot-die-coated PSCs, albeit at an uncertified 18.2% champion PCE [@10.1021/acsami.6c07537].
