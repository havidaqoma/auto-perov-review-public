Undercoordinated lead (Pb2+) sites and halide vacancies drive non-radiative recombination and initiate photochemical degradation. Multidentate Lewis bases, chelating additives, and biopolymers suppress these trap states by coordinating Pb2+ and arresting formamidinium (FA+) deprotonation or iodide oxidation [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02033] [@10.1002/adfm.78067] [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c02253] [@10.1002/anie.5667084] [@10.1021/acsami.6c13903] [@10.1002/adfm.77504]. Coordinating Pb2+ with phenylthiourea (TPU) retards ion migration by suppressing iodine vacancies and volatile iodine loss, achieving an uncertified champion power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 26.13% [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02033]. Ammonium alginate stabilizes FA+ against deprotonation and passivates Pb2+, increasing the fill factor (FF) from 80.2% to 85.1% in an uncertified 26.12% cell [@10.1002/adfm.78067]. Related treatments using sulfamethoxazole (SMX) (champion 21.05%, average 20.31%) [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c02253], a sulfonium-amino acid additive (champion 22.5%) [@10.1002/anie.5667084], multi-pyridyl spirobifluorenes (champion 20.98% versus 18.67% for control) [@10.1021/acsami.6c13903], and 3-(trifluoromethyl)thiophenol (22.30% PCE) [@10.1002/adfm.77504] similarly neutralize coordination defects, yet these studies examine uncertified small-area cells without establishing thermal bond durability.

Beyond surface coordination, mitigating ion migration requires addressing lattice mechanics, strain, and kinetic barriers within the perovskite absorber [@10.1093/nsr/nwag484] [@10.1002/aenm.71474] [@10.1002/aenm.71497] [@10.1002/aenm.71396] [@10.1002/adma.74538] [@10.1002/adfm.77855]. Stiffening the framework through solid-solution-induced lattice hardening via partial lanthanum substitution for Pb increases the Young's modulus from 27.96 to 35.71 GPa, delivering a certified steady-state efficiency of 26.03% (champion 26.54%) [@10.1093/nsr/nwag484]. Regulating crystallographic strain further restricts ion migration channels: shifting lattice strain from tensile to compressive with bimolecular sulfonates delivers an uncertified champion 26.21% PCE [@10.1002/aenm.71474], dynamic strain homogenization via supramolecular networks in tin-lead (Sn-Pb) perovskite solar cells (PSCs) preserves 84.7% efficiency after 450 h of maximum power point tracking (MPPT) (champion 24.1%) [@10.1002/aenm.71497], and doping with 0.8 mol% fluorinated methylammonium iodide (FMAI) maintains operational stability over 2000 h (champion 25.67%) [@10.1002/aenm.71396]. Extended defect formation presents an associated thermodynamic dilemma: dimethylammonium (DMA+) phase engineering suppresses Ruddlesden-Popper antiphase boundary (RP-APB) defects in beta-(DMA,Cs)PbI3 without nucleating the photo-inactive delta phase from edge-sharing [PbI6]4- octahedra (champion 20.23%) [@10.1002/adma.74538]. Defect healing also follows an explicit kinetic hierarchy: ordered anion-cation modification (OACM) demonstrates that anionic vacancies must be repaired before cation passivation to match bromide diffusion kinetics, enabling a 1.83 eV wide-bandgap (WBG) cell with an open-circuit voltage (Voc) of 1.33 V and 19.21% stabilized efficiency (champion 26.2%) [@10.1002/adfm.77855].

At device contacts, dimensional heterostructures and buried molecular interlayers block defect propagation and mobile ion accumulation [@10.1002/anie.8256065] [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15007814/v1] [@10.1002/smll.75536] [@10.1002/advs.76791] [@10.1007/s40820-026-02278-6] [@10.1002/smll.75360] [@10.1021/jacs.6c12975]. A tilted one-dimensional (1D) on three-dimensional (3D) heterointerface directed by a polyfluorinated ammonium iodide regulator preserves vertical transport while physically obstructing defect propagation and ion migration (champion 24.94% PCE) [@10.1002/anie.8256065]. In two-dimensional (2D) capping layers, octylammonium bromide (OABr) concentration dictates phase shifts toward higher n-values (champion 19.29% PCE) [@10.1002/advs.76791], whereas chemical polishing removes unreacted formamidinium iodide (FAI) and residual lead iodide (PbI2) to enforce vertical 2D orientation (champion 21.36% PCE) [@10.1002/smll.75536]. Solid-state halide exchange using bromide-rich nanocrystals passivates iodide vacancies and suppresses ion drift (uncertified preprint champion 22.19%) [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15007814/v1]. At buried electron-transport layer (ETL) interfaces, 2-formylbenzenesulfonic acid sodium salt (2-FAS) bridges self-assembled monolayers (SAM) to perovskite (champion 26.21% PCE, 86.15% FF) [@10.1007/s40820-026-02278-6], acesulfame potassium (Ace-K) chelates Sn4+ and oxygen vacancies on tin oxide (SnO2) to eliminate voids, retaining 92.4% efficiency after 1000 h of MPPT on an uncertified 0.0729 cm2 cell (champion 26.17%) [@10.1002/smll.75360], and transient lead thiocyanate (Pb(SCN)2) vapor eliminates residual PbI2 traps (champion 25.2%) [@10.1021/jacs.6c12975].
