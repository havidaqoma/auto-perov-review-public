Inhomogeneous nucleation triggers point defects, phase segregation, and structural disorder in metal halide perovskite absorbers, requiring precise crystallization modulation [@10.1002/adma.74388]. Controlling intermediate solvent-solute coordination dictates lattice orientation and residual stress: destabilizing solvent-PbI2 adducts through cooperative interactions between sodium hydroxyquinoline sulfonate and sodium aminonaphthalene sulfonate drives out-of-plane [100] growth while converting tensile strain into compressive strain, yielding an uncertified champion power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 26.21% [@10.1002/aenm.71474]. Similarly, dual-mode coordination with 4-chlorophenethylammonium (4Cl-PA) generates a macroporous PbI2 template that facilitates organic cation infiltration, extending carrier lifetime to 161 ns by time-resolved photoluminescence (TRPL) in uncertified 22.73% efficient devices [@10.1016/j.jcis.2026.141449]. In tin perovskites, intermediate-mediated stepwise crystallization during a pre-annealing static stage with fluorinated phenethylammonium alters the [SnI6]4- electronic landscape to suppress Sn2+ oxidation, reaching a 9.29% PCE [@10.1021/acsami.6c11057]. However, chemical coordination efficacy depends on the processing solvent: while SnF2 suppresses Sn2+ oxidation in dimethylformamide:dimethyl sulfoxide (DMF:DMSO), it offers limited antioxidant protection in DMSO-free DMF:2-methylaminoethanol (DMF:2MAP), where formamidinium tin iodide (FASnI3) films remain inherently Sn2+-rich [@10.1021/acsaem.6c02063].

Phase stability is equally bounded by thermodynamic competition and local defect equilibria [@10.1002/adma.74538] [@10.1002/aenm.71396] [@10.1002/advs.76791]. In cesium-based systems, excess PbI2 suppresses Ruddlesden-Popper antiphase boundaries (RP-APBs) but triggers edge-sharing [PbI6]4- motifs that nucleate the non-photoactive delta phase; dimethylammonium (DMA+) phase engineering circumvents this breakdown by forming beta-(DMA,Cs)PbI3 and intrinsically eliminating RP-APBs to achieve an uncertified 20.23% PCE [@10.1002/adma.74538]. Similarly, incorporating 0.8 mol% fluorinated methylammonium iodide (FMAI) stabilizes the perovskite lattice against thermal degradation in a 25.67% negative-intrinsic-positive (n-i-p) device, though the reported 2000 h operational stability lacks detailed testing protocols [@10.1002/aenm.71396]. In low-dimensional heterostructures, organic cation evaporation during annealing drives a dimensional shift from low- to high-n quantum wells, where precursor octylammonium bromide (OABr) concentration predetermines the initial n = 1 phase distribution in 19.29% wide-bandgap cells [@10.1002/advs.76791]. Replacing insulating organic spacers with semiconducting diketopyrrolopyrrole cations enables bidirectional charge transfer with inorganic [PbI4]2- slabs and yields electron mobilities up to 1.3 × 10−3 cm2 V−1 s−1, although functional solar cell metrics were omitted [@10.1002/adma.74681].

In lead-free multinary lattices, accessing target crystal phases requires overcoming severe kinetic and thermodynamic barriers [@10.1016/j.solener.2026.114961] [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15008057/v1] [@10.1038/s43246-026-01261-3]. Sequentially evaporating methylbismuth iodide (MBI) bilayers at substrate temperatures as low as 40 °C requires post-deposition annealing above 200 °C to yield a monoclinic C2/c phase with (111) texture, yet champion PCE reaches only 0.53% [@10.1016/j.solener.2026.114961]. In double perovskites, solid-state mechanosynthesis reveals strict ionic size selectivity: liquid assistance drives Rb+ into the A-site and Na+ into the B-site of Cs2AgInCl6 [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15008057/v1]. Conversely, solvent-free hydro-solution precipitation directly produces cubic Fm-3m Cs2AgBiBr6 without high-temperature annealing [@10.1038/s43246-026-01261-3], indicating that solution-phase ionic transport bypasses the kinetic barriers inherent to vapor-phase and solid-state transformations.
