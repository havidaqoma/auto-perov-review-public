At buried hole-selective contacts in inverted positive-intrinsic-negative (p-i-n) and tandem perovskite solar cells (PSCs), disordered molecular packing and aggregation in self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) cause severe pinhole shunts and energy misalignment. Suppressing SAM clustering while keeping anchoring sites accessible resolves these losses: non-anchoring additives like TTMB secure open binding sites to reach a certified power conversion efficiency (PCE) of 33.66% (33.66% stabilised) in two-terminal (2T) perovskite/silicon tandems, hydrogen-bond networks via glycinamide hydrochloride in [2-(3,6-dimethoxy-9H-carbazol-9-yl)ethyl]phosphonic acid (MeO-4PACz) yield 22.38% champion PCE in pure-iodide wide-bandgap cells, and DMPA-based multilayers achieve a certified 27.2% PCE (27.59% champion) with 94.5% retention after 1600 hours under continuous 1 Sun illumination at 65 °C [@10.1038/s41467-026-76266-0] [@10.1016/j.decarb.2026.100190] [@10.1038/s41467-026-76497-1]. Interfacial dipole engineering further optimizes band offsets: depositing bismuth oxychloride (BiOCl) onto SAMs induces dipole flipping and chlorine-iodine exchange, yielding a certified 27.84% 2T tandem PCE (28.14% champion, 27.86% stabilised), although single-junction efficiencies of 27.25% (1.55 eV) and 20.24% (1.85 eV) remain uncertified on small 0.09 cm² aperture areas [@10.1038/s41467-026-76786-9]. This aligns with co-assembling 4-ASA with Me-4PACz on nickel oxide (NiOx) to yield a 20.36% four-terminal (4T) tandem PCE (17.82% scanned single junction), and dipole tuning via m-FBC raising PCE from 23.05% to an uncertified 25.03%, though stability was tested only under 500 hours of uncharacterized storage [@10.1038/s41467-026-76847-z] [@10.1021/acsami.6c11467]. Asymmetric heteroatom conjugation also regulates contact energetics: S-AS on NiOx achieves 25.70% steady-state output (26.49% champion) and LW-1 on fluorine-doped tin oxide (FTO) reaches 26.0% stabilised efficiency (26.38% champion) [@10.1002/adfm.77712] [@10.1002/aenm.71482].

Beyond electrostatic alignment, buried contact molecules template crystallization, relieve lattice strain, and passivate undercoordinated lead (Pb²⁺) defects. Multi-arm donor-acceptor dipoles (T4IA) coordinate Pb²⁺ through C=N and methoxy groups to relieve residual strain (26.79% champion PCE), whereas non-covalent templating between precursor additives (TPANI) and structurally matched triphenylamine bisphosphonic acid SAMs on indium tin oxide (ITO) reduces intergranular groove depth and reinforces adhesion (26.58% champion) [@10.1002/anie.3716107] [@10.1002/anie.6932848]. Interfacial Pb-S bonding provides a parallel passivation route: thienyl-based SAM additives template preferential (111) crystallization and segregate at grain boundaries to secure a certified 32.56% (33.0% champion) 2T tandem PCE, mirroring solution-phase sulfidation with dibutyl sulfide that induces an interfacial p-to-n transition to yield an uncertified 26.28% PCE [@10.1002/anie.5241611] [@10.1021/acsami.6c07884].

Contact degradation also stems from chemical reactivity across metal oxide substrates. On NiOx hole transport layers (HTLs), removing deleterious surface hydroxyls with ammonium thiocyanate (NH4SCN) lowers transfer barriers and lattice strain (21.41% champion) [@10.1021/acsaem.6c01708]. Decoupling SAM anchoring from charge extraction through composite NiOx stacks (sputtered base plus solution-deposited hydroxyl overlayer) permits scalable processing, achieving 24.86% PCE across 12.4 cm², consistent with enamine SAM anchor engineering that achieves 23.14% PCE with 83.11% fill factor (FF) on a 29.7 cm² mini-module (>25.5% on small cells) [@10.1002/smll.75056] [@10.1002/advs.76950]. At tin oxide (SnO2) electron transport layers (ETLs) in regular negative-intrinsic-positive (n-i-p) devices, native surface hydroxyls and oxygen vacancies provoke interfacial void formation and microcracking; these are suppressed either by converting hydroxyls into covalently anchored acetate via ethanol vapor reconstruction (26.19% champion) or by subliming pyruvic acid at 145 °C to purge defect sites, reaching 26.17% PCE on 1 cm² with an 80% operational lifetime (T80) of 900 hours [@10.1002/smll.75122] [@10.1002/aenm.71514]. However, interfacial engineering reveals a trade-off between extraction and mechanical integrity: although fullerene SAMs (C60-PA) on SnO2 raise initial tracked efficiencies from 18.6% to a 20.0% stabilised value, performance degrades under illumination at elevated temperatures because fullerenes weaken adhesion at the oxide interface [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01485].
