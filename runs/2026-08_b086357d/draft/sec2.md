Independent certification establishes that spectral splitting across complementary bandgaps yields the highest verified efficiencies, driven by suppressed thermalization and optimized current matching in two-terminal (2T) perovskite tandems. Verified 2T power conversion efficiency (PCE) benchmarks reached a ceiling of 33.66%, followed by certified reports at 32.56%, 30.77%, 29.7%, and 27.84% [@10.1038/s41467-026-76266-0] [@10.1002/anie.5241611] [@10.1038/s41467-026-76713-y] [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102645] [@10.1038/s41467-026-76786-9]. Single-junction inverted (p-i-n) devices, constrained by non-radiative interfacial recombination and extraction barriers at selective contacts, established a narrower certified ceiling between 26.77% and 27.4%, led by verified values of 27.4%, 27.2%, and 26.77% [@10.1126/science.aeg8415] [@10.1038/s41467-026-76497-1] [@10.1002/anie.3095986]. Despite these verified milestones, rigorous certification remains rare across the literature: only 18 of 144 closely read papers reported an independently certified efficiency, allowing uncertified self-reported champion values to run ahead of verifiable baselines.

Translating these contact interfaces across larger dimensions exposes severe transport bottlenecks governed by transparent electrode sheet resistance, scribe line geometric dead area, and spatial film non-uniformity. At expanded dimensions, minimodule architectures maintained reported efficiencies of 27.7% on 25 cm2 and 27.01% on 58.51 cm2 [@10.1038/s43246-026-01321-8] [@10.1002/adma.74469]. However, larger apertures revealed significant performance losses, dropping to 23.3% on 57.3 cm2, 23.14% on 29.7 cm2, and 17.35% on 61.2 cm2 [@10.1002/adma.74629] [@10.1002/advs.76950] [@10.1002/smll.75068]. A champion value recorded on a sub-0.1 cm2 laboratory cell isolates microscopic interfacial transport under near-ideal current extraction, whereas an independently certified value measured across a module aperture gauges lateral conduction, spatial defect distributions, and coating reproducibility. These two metrics represent entirely distinct categories of physical evidence, meaning small-cell records cannot demonstrate module viability.
