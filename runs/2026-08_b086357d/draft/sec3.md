Defect formation and ion migration in metal halide perovskites are coupled through mobile halide vacancies that trigger operational degradation. Continuous illumination accelerates iodide oxidation to molecular iodine, generating iodine vacancies that expose undercoordinated lead (Pb2+) sites [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02033], whereas nonadiabatic molecular dynamics shows that iodine vacancies drive hydrogen vacancy formation into stable VI·VH defect complexes whose migration is immobilized by rigid Pb-C coordinate bonding in VI·VH-C configurations [@10.1021/acsnano.6c10992]. Kinetic and compositional regulation confirms that mobile halide management is prerequisite to suppressing ion drift [@10.1002/adfm.77855] [@10.1002/aenm.71462]. Exploiting the kinetic accessibility of bromide (Br-) diffusion, an ordered anion-cation modification (OACM) strategy repairs anionic vacancies before cation passivation, suppressing phase segregation to achieve a 1.33 V open-circuit voltage (Voc) and 19.21% stabilized efficiency in 1.83 eV wide-bandgap (WBG) top cells (champion 26.2% tandem efficiency) [@10.1002/adfm.77855]. Selenocyanate pseudohalide alloying similarly retards ion drift and homogenizes formamidinium (FA+) distributions throughout 1.66 eV absorbers [@10.1002/aenm.71462]. Structural barriers provide complementary confinement: scaffold-directed polyfluorinated 7F-EA-HI forms an interwoven tilted one-dimensional/three-dimensional (1D/3D) heterointerface that blocks defect propagation and ion migration in inverted (p-i-n) devices reaching an uncertified 24.94% power conversion efficiency (PCE) [@10.1002/anie.8256065], whereas self-driven halide exchange with bromide-rich nanocrystals passivates iodide vacancies via a surface-graded heterojunction, though restricted to an uncertified 22.19% champion PCE in an unreviewed preprint [@10.26434/chemrxiv.15007814/v1].

Beyond mobile halides, eliminating deep non-radiative traps demands coordinated passivation of undercoordinated metal cations and organic cation vacancies [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02033] [@10.1002/adfm.78067] [@10.1021/acsami.6c10456] [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c02253]. Bulk phenylthiourea coordinates Pb2+ to retard ion migration and suppress defect formation under illumination [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c02033]. Ammonium alginate suppresses FA+ deprotonation while passivating undercoordinated Pb2+, raising fill factor (FF) from 80.2% to 85.1% in an uncertified 26.12% champion cell [@10.1002/adfm.78067]. Dual-site coordination similarly targets opposing charges: sodium cyclamate coordinates Pb2+ via sulfonic acid groups and binds iodide vacancies through amino hydrogen bonds [@10.1021/acsami.6c10456], while multidentate sulfamethoxazole coordinates Pb2+, organic-cation, and halide vacancies, lifting normal (n-i-p) efficiency from 18.01% to an uncertified 21.05% champion (20.31% average) [@10.1021/acs.jpclett.6c02253]. Coupled hydrogen-bonding and electrostatic networks from the ionic liquid BSPH coordinate FA+, halides, and Pb2+, suppressing trap density and ion drift to yield a 23.72% p-i-n PCE [@10.1002/solr.70461]. In hole-conductor-free carbon-electrode mesoscopic cells, DMSC passivates both Pb and I species to reach a 22.5% champion PCE [@10.1002/anie.5667084], while multi-pyridyl spirobifluorenes coordinate defects during crystallization to yield 20.98% PCE versus 18.67% in controls [@10.1021/acsami.6c13903]. In tin-based absorbers prone to Sn2+ oxidation, triphenylphosphine oxide coordinates Sn2+ as a hard Lewis base, yielding 14.13% PCE and a T80 of 720 h under unlabelled testing conditions [@10.1002/solr.70454].

A third frontier links defect formation directly to lattice mechanics and buried transport contacts [@10.1093/nsr/nwag484] [@10.1002/aenm.71497] [@10.1002/smll.75360]. Solid-solution lattice hardening by partial lanthanum substitution of Pb raises the Young modulus from 27.96 to 35.71 GPa, demonstrating that rigidifying the inorganic cage suppresses defect formation to yield a 26.54% champion PCE and a certified steady-state efficiency of 26.03% [@10.1093/nsr/nwag484]. Dynamic strain homogenization via in situ supramolecular networks modulates Sn2+ coordination in mixed tin-lead absorbers, suppressing segregation to retain 84.7% initial efficiency after 450 h maximum power point tracking (MPPT) under one sun and 92.1% after 50 thermal cycles (24.1% champion PCE) [@10.1002/aenm.71497]. At buried contacts, Ace-K bidentately chelates uncoordinated Sn4+ and oxygen vacancies on tin dioxide (SnO2) while outcompeting dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO) for lead iodide (PbI2), eliminating voids to retain 92.4% efficiency after 1000 h MPPT on a 0.0729 cm2 cell (26.17% champion PCE) [@10.1002/smll.75360]. Interfacial molecular bridging with 2-formylbenzenesulfonic acid sodium salt connects self-assembled monolayers (SAM) to perovskite, delivering an uncertified 26.21% champion PCE with an 86.15% FF [@10.1007/s40820-026-02278-6]. At module scale, potassium dihydrogen phosphate disaggregates SnO2 colloids and passivates buried contacts, yielding 23.16% PCE on 12 cm2 modules with 8% scaling loss from a 25.34% champion cell [@10.1002/aenm.71419].
