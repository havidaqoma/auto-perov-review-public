Non-conformal coverage and dewetting of hole-selective self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) on textured silicon substrates generate interfacial voids and unreacted lead iodide (PbI2), driving non-radiative recombination in two-terminal (2T) perovskite/silicon tandems [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102645] [@10.1002/aenm.71512] [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01966]. Resolving this buried morphological mismatch requires tailored nucleation and crystallization: a CsCl seed layer facilitates organic precursor incorporation across MeO-2PACz to suppress interfacial PbI2, yielding a certified 29.7% power conversion efficiency (PCE) [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102645], while solvent-free sequential evaporation capitalizes on accelerated lead halide conversion on textured silicon to deliver a 27.0% stabilized PCE [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102646]. Post-deposition methylammonium thiocyanate (MASCN) healing similarly repairs valley-confinement defects by driving secondary crystal growth into full-thickness columnar grains, achieving a certified 30.77% PCE on a 1.164 cm2 aperture and retaining initial performance after 3400 hours of continuous ambient maximum power point tracking (MPPT) [@10.1038/s41467-026-76713-y]. In the wide-bandgap (WBG) top absorber, phase segregation and halide migration govern bulk degradation. Elevating the ion migration energy barrier improves local structural stability, supporting an uncertified champion PCE of 33.02% [@10.1002/adfm.77637], while two-dimensional (2D) (APD)PbI4 seed templates impart regular compressive strain into the three-dimensional (3D) lattice to suppress phase separation for a 29.47% PCE [@10.1021/acsnano.6c07355]. Contact durability under subsequent processing presents a further constraint: sacrificial HTFNA coordination shields SAMs against atomic layer deposition (ALD) damage, yet this certified 26.56% efficiency is restricted to a 0.062 cm2 cell [@10.1002/adma.74461], highlighting the shift toward resilient inorganic contacts like nickel oxide with N719 dye that preserve 96.4% PCE after one year of outdoor MPPT [@10.1021/acsenergylett.6c01363]. Translating these interfaces to large-area modules escalates multi-scale defect densities [@10.1002/adfm.202532038], requiring kinetic crystallization control: polyfluoroarene additives elevate effective nucleation barriers to yield a 27.01% module PCE across 58.51 cm2 with 55 mV non-radiative voltage loss [@10.1002/adma.74469], matched by static antisolvent engineering achieving 27.7% on 25 cm2 [@10.1038/s43246-026-01321-8].
