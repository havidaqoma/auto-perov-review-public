Controlling wide-bandgap (WBG) perovskite crystallization across textured silicon substrates represents the most supported mechanism this month, advancing certified two-terminal (2T) power conversion efficiency (PCE) to 33.66% [@10.1038/s41467-026-76266-0] and 30.77% [@10.1038/s41467-026-76713-y]. However, the assembled evidence leaves critical physical questions unestablished across four distinct areas.

First, although seed layers and secondary grain growth suppress phase segregation during deposition [@10.1038/s41467-026-76713-y] [@10.1016/j.joule.2026.102645], current data do not demonstrate that columnar grains prevent electric-field-driven halide redistribution under sustained bias. Operando depth-profiling time-of-flight secondary ion mass spectrometry (ToF-SIMS) under operational voltage bias is required to determine whether seeded interfaces permanently impede ion migration or merely delay initial demixing. Second, while non-anchoring additives reduce aggregation in self-assembled monolayers (SAMs) [@10.1038/s41467-026-76266-0], the literature fails to verify whether these modified contacts survive wet chemical processing: acidic phosphonic acid SAMs trigger dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO)-mediated iodide redox reactions during ink coating [@10.1126/science.aeg8415], leaving uncertified champion efficiencies vulnerable to severe certification discrepancies [@10.1002/adma.74438]. In situ grazing-incidence wide-angle X-ray scattering (GIWAXS) coupled with interface-sensitive photoelectron spectroscopy during solution wetting must establish whether molecular additives protect buried contacts from chemical degradation. Third, device scalability remains disconnected from small-area interfacial gains: scale-up was addressed by 27 of 531 works, with the largest reported aperture reaching 61.2 cm2 [@10.1002/smll.75068], yet reported data do not decouple the physical origins of area-dependent efficiency penalties [@10.1038/s41467-026-76425-3]. Calibrated absolute electroluminescence imaging resolving spatial ideality factors across square-decimeter modules is necessary to separate interconnect sheet resistance from spatial non-radiative recombination. Fourth, operational longevity remains unverified across the field. Only 5 papers reported a time to 80% of initial performance (T80) lifetime, with the longest reaching 1400 h [@10.1002/anie.6548693], and only 2 papers named an International Summit on Organic Photovoltaic Stability (ISOS) protocol [@10.1002/anie.4221605] [@10.1038/s41467-026-76848-y]. Standardized ISOS testing combining elevated thermal stress with continuous maximum power point tracking (MPPT) under full illumination is needed to isolate contact delamination from intrinsic lattice decay.

The certified reporting share was 8.3 in 2026-07 and 9.7 in 2026-08, yet only 18 of 144 closely read papers reported any certified value. Requiring standardized ISOS protocols alongside certified aperture-area efficiencies would make next month's literature directly comparable across laboratories.
